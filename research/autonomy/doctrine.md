# China's autonomy doctrine: what public sources actually establish

Research cutoff and retrieval: 2026-09-10. Prepared for the PLA Observatory autonomy research extension. Primary official sources only. Translations below are analytical translations unless explicitly identified otherwise. This is a public-source doctrine-and-concepts review, not an account of classified rules or a deployment inventory.

## Principal findings

The public record supports a modernization direction much more confidently than a detailed operational doctrine. National documents direct intelligentization, unmanned forces, integrated information systems and civil–military technology transfer. A CMC announcement establishes that a binding joint-operations outline exists; the announcement does not disclose autonomy provisions. Signed PLA Daily articles supply useful conceptual detail, but should be coded as professional advocacy or analysis rather than adopted PLA doctrine.

Human authority and machine autonomy are separate dimensions. Official policy calls for human control and ultimate human responsibility. That does not establish continuous manual control of every task, the approval required for a particular action, or a tested ability to interrupt a deployed system. Those require system-specific evidence.

The most useful research object is **a bounded function under a specified allocation of authority**, alongside the evidence that it works. A platform can automate locomotion while relying on a person for scene interpretation, task assignment or recovery. An intelligent command-support system can matter militarily without being a physical robot. Treat unmanned, autonomous, intelligent and embodied as different attributes.

There is no sound basis here for one national autonomy ladder. One signed 2024 article uses loop terminology in an unusual order, and its own highest-autonomy category still includes human monitoring and takeover. Preserve source wording and code concrete human functions separately.

## Eight core source records

### D01 — 2019 national-defense white paper

- **Title:** 《新时代的中国国防》 / China's National Defense in the New Era.
- **Issuer/status:** State Council Information Office; national policy white paper, reproduced by MOD/Xinhua. Not a platform specification or operational regulation.
- **Date:** 2019-07-24.
- **URL:** https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/16092823.html
- **Locator:** Part I, subsection 国际军事竞争日趋激烈; Part II discussion of defense modernization.
- **Short original:** 智能化战争初现端倪
- **Translation:** “Intelligentized warfare is beginning to emerge.”
- **Bounded finding:** Describes AI and other digital technologies as drivers of changing warfare. It also acknowledges contemporary modernization deficiencies and makes further intelligentization a development objective. Read its statements about deficiencies as 2019 assessments, not measurements of 2026 capability.
- **Does not establish:** Fielded autonomous capability, human-authorization procedures, supplier relationships or a particular robotics architecture.
- **Evidence coding:** `national_policy`; `development_direction`; `capability_unmeasured`.

### D02 — CMC Joint Operations Outline promulgation

- **Title:** 经中央军委主席习近平批准 中央军委印发《中国人民解放军联合作战纲要（试行）》.
- **Issuer/status:** CMC promulgation reported by Xinhua on MOD. Announcement of an actual military regulation; the retrieved page is not the outline's full text.
- **Date:** Announcement 2020-11-13; effective 2020-11-07.
- **URL:** https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/4874081.html?big=fan
- **Locator:** Third substantive paragraph describes regulatory status and scope; final paragraph specifies its use for joint operations and joint training.
- **Short original:** 顶层法规
- **Translation:** “Top-level regulation.”
- **Bounded finding:** The announcement identifies a binding framework for joint command, operations, support and related responsibilities and procedures. It is the crucial example of what a formal doctrinal/regulatory source looks like.
- **Does not establish:** What the inaccessible outline says about autonomous systems. Do not fill that gap using newspaper commentary and then attribute the result to the outline.
- **Evidence coding:** `regulation_announcement`; `full_text_not_reviewed`; `autonomy_content_unknown`.

### D03 — Military AI position paper

- **Title:** 中国关于规范人工智能军事应用的立场文件.
- **Issuer/status:** PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs; declared international policy position, not disclosed PLA operating instructions.
- **Date:** 2021-12-14.
- **Chinese URL:** https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/ziliao_674904/zcwj_674915/202112/t20211214_10469511.shtml
- **Official English:** https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/wjb/zzjg_663340/jks_665232/kjlc_665236/AI/202112/t20211214_10469512.html
- **Locator:** Bullets 技术安全上 and 研发操作上.
- **Short original:** 人类是最终责任主体
- **Translation:** “Humans are the ultimate responsible actors.”
- **Bounded finding:** Calls for relevant weapons to remain under human control, human ability to suspend operation, lifecycle human–machine interaction, accountability and operator training. These are distinct policy commitments and should not be compressed into a claim that every decision requires individual human approval.
- **Does not establish:** Implemented control interfaces, response times, authorization thresholds or compliance in a named system.
- **Evidence coding:** `declared_international_policy`; `human_responsibility`; `implementation_unverified`.

### D04 — Arms-control white paper updates the human-control position

- **Title:** 《新时代的中国军控、裁军与防扩散》.
- **Issuer/status:** State Council Information Office white paper, full text on MFA; national policy.
- **Date:** 2025-11-27.
- **URL:** https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/wjb_673085/zzjg_673183/jks_674633/jksxwlb_674635/202511/t20251127_11761606.shtml
- **Locator:** Part IV, subsection 三 / 人工智能军事应用; paragraph beginning 倡导以人为本、智能向善发展理念. Browser text lines 287–293 contain the subsection.
- **Short original:** 防止非授权行动
- **Translation:** “Prevent unauthorized actions.”
- **Bounded finding:** Reiterates human primacy, ultimate human responsibility and human control of relevant weapon systems. Also presents safety, quality control and risk management as development concerns. This is a newer official anchor than relying solely on the 2021 paper.
- **Does not establish:** That the declared principles have been implemented consistently, or which authorization boundary applies to any particular function.
- **Evidence coding:** `national_policy`; `human_authority`; `implementation_unverified`.

### D05 — Approved 15th Five-Year Plan connects autonomy to the wider industrial system

- **Title:** 中华人民共和国国民经济和社会发展第十五个五年规划纲要.
- **Issuer/status:** National development plan approved by the NPC; authoritative development policy, not an operational manual.
- **Dates:** Approved 2026-03-12; authorized full-text publication 2026-03-13. MOD page timestamp 2026-03-13 22:53:37.
- **URL:** https://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/qwfb/yw_214049/16448575.html
- **Opened authorized full-text mirror:** https://www.news.cn/politics/20260313/085af5de5a4b4268aa7d87d90817df2f/c.html (Xinhua, 2026-03-13; chapters 55–56 inspected).
- **Approval/publication confirmation:** https://www.locpg.gov.cn/20260313/c28ce62400224e88a0fd30df6bb394f0/c.html
- **Locator:** Part XV; chapter 55, section 1 (加快先进战斗力建设); chapter 56 (巩固提高一体化国家战略体系和能力). For civilian digital foundations, chapter 12 covers compute, algorithms and data.
- **Short original:** 加快无人智能作战力量及反制能力建设
- **Translation:** “Accelerate development of unmanned intelligent forces and countermeasures.”
- **Bounded finding:** Places unmanned/intelligent force development alongside information-system integration, data use, training and modernization of the larger military system. The following chapter calls for frontier civilian technology transfer channels, procurement improvements and greater compatibility of civil/military standards.
- **Does not establish:** A funded contract, named supplier, technical maturity or actual transfer of a civilian model into a military system.
- **Evidence coding:** `national_plan`; `development_direction`; `transfer_policy_not_transaction`.

### D06 — Loop vocabulary is not a stable capability taxonomy

- **Title:** 无人作战亦有“人” / “Unmanned operations still involve people.”
- **Authors/status:** 姜相争、耿涛; signed PLA Daily commentary on China Military Online. Not a promulgated standard.
- **Date:** 2024-03-28.
- **URL:** https://www.81.cn/yw_208727/16297063.html
- **Locator:** The three consecutive body paragraphs headed by human on/in/out of the loop.
- **Short original:** 人在回路上
- **Translation:** “Human on the loop” — preserve the source's wording.
- **Bounded finding:** Its first category describes remote operation. Its next describes autonomous execution with human intervention at key transitions. Its last describes extensive autonomous activity constrained by human-set rules, with monitoring and possible takeover. Thus even this article's out-of-loop language does not mean humans disappear from governance. These are the authors' stages, not verified deployment levels.
- **Does not establish:** A nationally standardized meaning for the English loop categories. An analyst should record who authorizes, monitors, intervenes and recovers, instead of assigning a score from the label alone.
- **Evidence coding:** `signed_commentary`; `source_specific_taxonomy`; `prospective`.

### D07 — Task-dependent allocation of human and machine functions

- **Title:** 军事智能促进战斗力生成路径探析.
- **Authors/status:** 孔睿、仇昊、陈小迁; signed PLA Daily Military Forum article.
- **Date:** 2026-01-15.
- **URL:** https://www.81.cn/yw_208727/16437531.html
- **Official mirror:** https://www.81.mil.cn/yw_208727/16437531.html
- **Locator:** Paragraph beginning 在指挥控制领域形成人谋机算的高效指挥模式; later paragraph headed 深化人机融合新形态.
- **Short original:** 人谋机算
- **Translation:** “Human judgment and planning, machine calculation.”
- **Bounded finding:** Advocates complementary roles for commanders and computational systems, and adjustable allocation of authority depending on the task, environment and equipment maturity. The authors favor greater machine latitude for structured tasks while retaining human supervision and final judgment for consequential or ambiguous decisions. This gives analysts a useful question about conditions and authority, rather than evidence that a particular capability has been achieved.
- **Does not establish:** An adopted command policy, an implemented software architecture or operational performance.
- **Evidence coding:** `signed_commentary`; `proposed_function_allocation`; `implementation_unverified`.

### D08 — Military agents are described as hardware, software and rules

- **Title:** 把握军事智能体建设规律.
- **Authors/status:** 孔睿、陈小迁、孙鹏浩; signed PLA Daily article, published on China Military Online.
- **Date:** 2026-08-19.
- **URL:** https://www.81.cn/yw_208727/16480072.html
- **Official mirror:** https://www.81.mil.cn/yw_208727/16480072.html
- **Locator:** Four sections: 厘清军事智能体定义特征; 夯实军事智能体技术底座; 创新军事智能体运用模式; 规避军事智能体风险隐患.
- **Short original:** 人在回路、智能支撑
- **Translation:** “Humans in the loop, supported by machine intelligence.”
- **Bounded finding:** Defines the subject broadly across hardware, algorithms and interaction rules; presents algorithms, compute, data and physical platforms as interdependent. Calls for explicit allocation of human/system responsibilities and addresses loss of control, accountability and human skill erosion. Its prescriptions for data management and integration identify research priorities, not completed capacities.
- **Does not establish:** A standardized PLA agent architecture, deployment numbers, safe autonomy or tested performance. Repeated authorship with D07 is intellectual continuity, not independent confirmation.
- **Evidence coding:** `signed_commentary`; `conceptual_stack`; `development_agenda`.

## Terminology to retain in the analyst product

| Chinese term | Working English | Coding caution |
|---|---|---|
| 无人化 | Unmanned/uncrewed operation | Absence of an onboard person says little about remote labor or decision authority. |
| 自主性 | Autonomy | Attach to a function and conditions; avoid a platform-wide binary. |
| 智能化 | Intelligentization | A modernization concept broader than robotics or generative AI; it is not a measured autonomy level. |
| 人机协同 | Human–machine collaboration | Specify the actual division of work and responsibility. |
| 有人无人协同 | Manned–unmanned / crewed–uncrewed teaming | Describes participating systems; does not by itself establish machine autonomy. |
| 人谋机算 | Human judgment/planning with machine calculation | D07's conceptual division, not a universal control standard. |
| 军事智能体 | Military intelligent agent | In D08, a broad system concept; do not silently equate it to an LLM agent. |
| 自主可控 | Independently controllable / under domestic control, depending on context | In industrial policy passages often concerns technological dependence; do not automatically translate as autonomous robot behavior. |

These are analytical working distinctions, not a claim that all authors use these terms consistently.

## How this should change the dataset

Maintain separate fields for `authority_class`, `issuer_or_author`, `publication_date`, `event_or_effective_date`, `source_term`, `source_definition`, `functional_claim`, `human_role`, `evidence_stage`, `conditions`, and `unknowns`. A link to a document is insufficient without the particular section and the claim it supports.

Use evidence stages such as policy objective → conceptual proposal → technical publication → demonstration → acceptance → routine operation. These are categories of evidence, not an inevitable linear progression. A system can have strong locomotion evidence and no evidence for its task understanding or supervisory burden.

For human roles, capture task authorization, continuous teleoperation, exception intervention, outcome review, override, recovery and maintenance independently. Keep formal responsibility separate from practical workload. This connects doctrine to the economics dataset without claiming that commercial labor measurements establish military readiness.

The informative civ–mil research question is whether the same technical artifact, team, interface, dataset process or qualified component crosses the boundary, with a dated relationship that can be documented. A policy favoring transfer is only context. A company that uses similar vocabulary is not thereby a military supplier.

## Collection priorities

1. Public standards or acceptance documents defining human authority, exception handling and auditability for a named system. They would be more probative than another concept article.
2. Institutional provenance: distinguish CMC regulation, government plan, military research publication, signed newspaper commentary, training report and vendor marketing. Track common authors and republication to avoid false corroboration.
3. Demonstrated function and conditions: what was actually automated, what human work remained, and whether evaluation included failures and recovery. Avoid extrapolating a demonstration to routine use.
4. Evidence connecting the physical platform to software, data and integration suppliers. Preserve unknown integration relationships.
5. Changes over time in responsibility language and validation demands. Do not infer a policy reversal from one differently worded signed article.

Common publishers, shared authors and republication are not independent corroboration. The sourcebook preserves bounded claims and short excerpts rather than republishing complete articles.

No accessible full text of the 2020 Joint Operations Outline was reviewed. None of these eight sources independently measures a named system's deployed autonomy. This is the central limit to retain when using the word doctrine.
