# Documented transfer cases: ownership, software and the limit of the inference

Reviewed 10 September 2026. Primary issuer disclosures only. Relevant PDF pages read in full; the Jingpin R&D table headers/rows and Xinhongye ownership/consolidation tables were visually checked. No registry lookup or customer acceptance records obtained.

## Lead case: Estun exits Shuguang ownership; Xinhongye controls it and retains a prospective cooperation route

**Analyst judgment:** This establishes an organizational route linking civilian industrial automation and defense-related servo capabilities. It does not establish transfer of an autonomous navigation model, training dataset, common software release, or accepted military autonomous system. Ending equity ownership also does not demonstrate the end of all technical or supply ties.

### Source A — signed transaction terms, not just an announced ambition

[无锡鑫宏业线缆科技股份有限公司：关于签订《股权转让协议》暨交易框架协议的进展公告，2025-085](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-10-20/1224720253.PDF), signed 20 October 2025.

- pp1,7: Xinhongye agreed to purchase 62% of **扬州曙光光电自控有限责任公司**: 48 percentage points from **南京鼎控机电科技有限公司** and 14 from **曙光蓝风启（南京）科技合伙企业（有限合伙）**. Contract consideration: RMB316.20m, including RMB244.80m for Dingkong’s portion.
- p4: Shuguang identifier **91321091743733424L**; this is the issuer-disclosed legal identity, not a fresh registry check.
- p9, section 五（三）: the buyer, **南京埃斯顿自动化股份有限公司**, and Shuguang intend cooperation in industrial robotics and embodied intelligence, with detailed terms to be separately agreed. Short excerpt: “具体合作细节…另行签订协议” — details require a separate agreement.
- p10, 五（四）4: transaction provisions require subsequent changes/filings for defense-industry and secrecy qualifications. Excerpt: “国防军工、保密资质”. This supports defense-business relevance; it does not itself prove completed requalification.

**Stage:** signed equity and prospective strategic-cooperation terms. Keep the ownership closing separate from implementation of cooperation.

### Source B — buyer-side closing and functional description

[Xinhongye FY2025 annual report, 2026-024](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-17/1225109422.PDF), published 17 April 2026; reporting period ends 31 December 2025.

- pp194–195, financial statements IX.1: 62% acquired; accounting purchase/control date **4 November 2025**; recorded acquisition cost **RMB314.34m**. Shuguang revenue from acquisition date to year-end: **RMB33,496,772.42**. These are accounting measures; do not substitute the recorded cost for the contract price or annualize the short-period revenue.
- pp20–21, special-equipment servo systems: Shuguang develops/produces servo controllers, optomechanical-electronic systems, mechanical-microelectronic control systems and optical instruments. Management claims synergy with Xinhongye’s industrial-robot/ship cables.
- p21, humanoid-robot paragraph: Xinhongye separately reports providing specialized cables to **北京星动纪元科技有限公司** for joint-drive and dexterous-hand locations. It does not attribute that relationship to Shuguang or identify a shared servo product.

**Date reconciliation:** the existing Estun source records disposal completion on **3 November 2025**. Preserve the seller’s disposal date and buyer’s accounting control date as separate observations rather than silently changing either.

### Source C — currentness through the next reporting period

[Xinhongye H1 2026 report, 2026-045](https://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-8/2026-08-20/12504852.PDF), issuer PDF mirrored by Sina, published 20 August 2026; reporting period ends 30 June 2026. [Announcement page](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12504852&stockid=301310).

- Printed p130 (PDF page 131), X.1: Xinhongye directly holds **62%** of Shuguang. It indirectly holds **62%** of **扬州曙光软件技术有限公司** and **扬州寰宇软件技术有限公司**, both classified as software businesses.
- Printed p15 (PDF page 16): Shuguang’s servo/system business description continues.

**Important distinction:** an acquired software legal entity is evidence of organizational capability and ownership. Its name and industry classification do not establish that its software is AI, autonomous navigation, military decision-making, or shared with civilian customers.

### What changes in the existing dossier

1. Retain historical Estun ownership dates and add a new Xinhongye→Shuguang 62% controlling relationship, observed through 30 June 2026.
2. Add a distinct prospective Estun–Xinhongye–Shuguang cooperation relationship, dated 20 October 2025. Label its implementation unverified. It is contrary evidence to any blanket claim that all connections ended with disposal.
3. Separate cables, servo control, software subsidiaries and high-level autonomy. These are different artifacts/functions and cannot inherit one another’s maturity or customer relationships.
4. Avoid claiming that Xinhongye’s named humanoid customer buys Shuguang defense servos. The reviewed disclosure does not connect those edges.

**Most discriminating next evidence:** the separate cooperation agreement envisaged in section 五（三), dated post-sale supply/service contracts, or an identified commercial software/component release shared across named applications. Absence of these documents from this review is not proof they do not exist.

## Additional concrete autonomy case: Jingpin’s warehouse software has dated progress, but its tender language is a target

### Source D — FY2025 baseline

[北京晶品特装科技股份有限公司 FY2025 annual report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-28/1225210584.PDF), published 28 April 2026.

- p14: the issuer describes defense/public-safety robots and civilian manufacturing/warehouse activities; upstream components remain described as internally used and potentially externally saleable after adaptation.
- p21, R&D item 3: new warehouse-control scheduler integrates several equipment types; historical orders inform simulation at **5,000 simulated orders/day**. No numeric utilization improvement or actual customer-site throughput is disclosed there.
- pp25–26, R&D table, row14 **LTKY-202501**: progress is completion of four-direction and six-direction vehicle modules. A working WMS test installation and winning an important tender appear in the **拟达到目标** column (intended objectives), not achieved progress.
- p33: **12,248** special-robot sales is a G001 standard-labor-hour equivalent, not delivered robot count.

### Source E — H1 2026 update

[Jingpin H1 2026 report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-25/1225497124.PDF), published 25 August 2026, explicitly unaudited.

- pp10–12,15: management describes DMS, WCS and MES software for warehouse/transfer/inbound workflow, plus warehouse equipment. These are named system classes, not versioned public releases.
- pp16–17, R&D table, row10 **LTKY-202501**: achieved progress is “完成软件测试、更新” — software testing and updates completed. The WMS test-environment/tender statement remains in **拟达到目标**. The report does not establish acceptance at a named customer site for this project.
- p4: **上海料顿智能科技有限公司** is a controlled subsidiary; p26 attributes warehouse/logistics R&D, production and sales to it and reports H1 revenue **RMB2.6267m**. Do not attribute all subsidiary revenue to LTKY-202501.
- p20 acknowledges that civilian-market development may fall short of expectations.

**Analyst inference:** This is a dated, identifiable commercial coordination-software effort inside a defense-related robotics group. It is stronger evidence than a generic military-to-civilian slogan, but still does not demonstrate shared military/civil software lineage, field economics or autonomous authority over a military task. The next step is an accepted customer deployment tied to a software version, workflow and actual operating record.
