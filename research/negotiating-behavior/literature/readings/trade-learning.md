# Trade learning: full-text reading and primary-source checks

Read on 8 September 2026. This memo follows two complete scholarly works: Gregory Shaffer and Henry Gao, [“China’s Rise: How It Took On the U.S. at the WTO”](https://www.illinoislawreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Shaffer.pdf), *University of Illinois Law Review* 2018(1), 115–184; and Henry Gao, [“China’s Changing Perspective on the WTO: From Aspiration, Assimilation to Alienation”](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009291804.005), in *China and the WTO: A Twenty-Year Assessment* (2023), 45–70. The latter is the chapter version of the shortlisted 2022 article, not a second independent study. All 96 PDF pages, including footnotes, were read sequentially. The evidence file records versions, hashes, coverage, visual checks and access failures.

The defensible update is **a wider negotiating repertoire, with its deployment conditioned by institution, issue and authority**. It is premature to substitute a story of national legal conversion, or national abandonment of law, for Solomon’s account. Our 1999 pilot already contains detailed schedules, conditional packages, competing bureaucratic mandates and political principles. Any later claim of change must identify what became more common, which actors acquired a new capability, and what they could do with it.

## What these readings contribute

Shaffer and Gao provide process evidence: 40 formal interviews with over 60 participants, supplemented by observation and documentary research. Their interview population concentrates on trade officials and internationally engaged professionals/businesses, a limitation expressly acknowledged at 123–124. Their accounts connect training, outside counsel and interagency work; they also report growing skepticism before 2017 (169–176). These are reasons to investigate institutional variation, not to treat interviewees as a representative sample of China’s leadership. [Full article](https://www.illinoislawreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Shaffer.pdf).

Gao’s later chapter assembles official statements, memoirs, legal disputes and policy analysis to interpret changing attitudes. Its periodization ends principally in 2021–early 2022 despite publication in 2023. The account of alienation is an interpretation; the chapter does not supply a new systematic interview sample or internal leadership decision files. Its proposed causal link from others’ conduct to Chinese behavior needs episode-level testing. [Chapter](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009291804.005), 45–70.

Our principal inference is that **expertise, compliance, confidence in an institution, and willingness to compromise are four separate variables**. A negotiator may become better at drafting claims while losing confidence that a favorable decision will change market access. A ministry may value a rule as an argument against another ministry while accepting political instructions that defeat its advice. A government may negotiate new rules in one forum and retaliate outside another. None of those combinations is conceptually inconsistent.

This improves the research design. Litigation counts are useful for selecting episodes, but cannot measure bargaining sophistication by themselves. We should observe who proposed a claim, who selected counsel, who approved settlement, which options lawyers ruled out, and whether officials anticipated domestic implementation problems. A successful ruling can result from stronger legal work, clearer underlying law, commercial pressure or the opponent’s weak defense. The relevant evidence must discriminate among these explanations.

## Three primary checks that materially change the questions

**1. Leadership sponsorship of expertise coexisted with a political interpretation of accession.** The complete published text of Jiang Zemin’s 25 February 2002 speech, *在激烈的国际竞争中掌握主动*, was read at 442–460, including editorial notes. It is identified as the main portion of a speech to provincial and ministerial leaders; it is not an unedited transcript. Pages 453–455 explicitly connect government reform, WTO rules and professional training. Pages 446–450 set these within national security, power and bargaining considerations. [Official collected speech, university-hosted PDF](https://djw.shufe-zj.edu.cn/__local/2/F7/B0/E678A3B1097BF28A7D08985B300_E8648320_7C80E6.pdf?e=.pdf); [Theory China edition, 447](https://ebook.theorychina.org.cn/ebook/upload/storage/files/2022/07/28/ed40ae0daae94e407f7fa64941facb3991619/files/basic-html/page459.html).

The methodological implication is substantial. This primary text prevents us from defining the early WTO period as simple faith in law subsequently replaced by strategic calculation. It instead supplies simultaneous instructions whose relative weight could change. To demonstrate evolution, recover training implementation and actual decisions, and ask which instruction prevailed when openness and security appeared to conflict. A public leadership text establishes an authorized account and policy direction; it does not establish every participant’s belief.

**2. The early defensive position already used precise legal design.** China’s 15 May 2003 communication, circulated 19 May as **TN/DS/W/57**, was read in full (three pages). It elaborates a proposed ceiling on developed members’ cases against individual developing members, rules for counting panels, asymmetric cost recovery, and shorter trade-remedy proceedings with a developing-respondent exception. It explicitly invokes resource and experience constraints. This is a proposal, not adopted WTO law. The copy is the original WTO-format document preserved by the Japan Machinery Center for Trade and Investment. [Full document](https://jmcti.org/2000round/com/doha/tn/tn_ds_w_057.pdf).

Calling that evidence merely “reluctance to litigate” would discard much of its value. Our inference is that we should examine the simultaneous desire to limit exposure and use adjudication against foreign trade barriers. The document does not tell us whether resource scarcity was the actual decisive motive, an advantageous justification, or both. Compare the initial instructions, successive 2003 drafts and counterpart reactions before claiming a transition from cultural aversion to sophisticated legal behavior.

**3. Formal compliance machinery continued developing after the scholarly observation window.** The entire published 2014 notice, including its attachment, was read. It requires departments to assess trade-policy compliance, while making consultation with MOFCOM conditional on the drafting department considering it necessary. The 17 June date cited in the 2018 article should not replace the document’s **9 June 2014** issuance date. [国办发〔2014〕29号](https://sms.mofcom.gov.cn/myzchggz/zdxwj/art/2014/art_abdaedf07c69499292211797d9deb1cc.html).

The full publicly released **16 March 2025** opinion was also read. It explicitly repeals the 2014 notice and requires pre-issuance assessment, written findings with submissions for approval, necessary changes where incompatibility risk is found, training and supervision. Its public version expressly states that material has been omitted. [国办发〔2025〕12号](https://sms.mofcom.gov.cn/gzdt/art/2025/art_f434b3faf5c5401b920ab121a2fa1772.html). MOFCOM’s accompanying explanation was read separately and treated as the issuing agency’s account of the revision. [21 March 2025 explanation](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcjd/dwmy/art/2025/art_a463d001ac3f4c3fa837d5d385b49651.html).

This warrants a new implementation test, not a finding of effective compliance. Seek completed assessments, policies changed following assessment, cases referred upward, and accountability decisions. Compare central and local practice. The published system leaves evaluation primarily with policy drafters; its wording alone does not establish an independent legal veto. The distinction between policy enactment and enforcement is indispensable for a 2026 update.

## What stays, what needs qualification

**Freeman’s learning propositions deserve expansion rather than rediscovery:** `F07` and `F13` already anticipate multilateral learning and institution-specific styles. `F12` provides a hypothesis about how experience passes between generations. The new task is to trace mechanisms and limits: recruitment, secondments, counsel relationships, repeated participation, and whether experience survives personnel transfers.

**Broad-principles claims need an explicit scope condition:** `S-P05` and `S-C13` cannot serve as default descriptions of all Chinese economic negotiation. Compare the types and functions of documents. A political principle can authorize a deal while a technical schedule determines its content; these are compatible. Our [1999 Chinese reconstruction](../../cases/wto-1999-chinese-reconstruction.md) gives an unusually strong baseline because the April MOU and November offers are actual documents. It would be misleading to make 2001 the first appearance of detailed Chinese bargaining.

**Authority and implementation remain central hypotheses:** `S-C10–12`, `S-P24–25` and `F09–11` tell us where to look, but should not predetermine the explanation. Lawyers may alter the choices placed before a leader without gaining authority to choose among them. Implementation can create renewed bargaining over deadlines, remedies and the meaning of a commitment. Our question is how legal institutions change that bargaining, not whether bargaining disappears.

**Interest-based explanations remain essential:** `S-C18` guards against mistaking tactics for causes. Test the same procedural behavior across cases with different commercial stakes and power balances. Add counterpart behavior to the chronology. A Chinese adaptation may reflect learning from an American action, independent domestic change, or a response to altered material incentives. Similar timing is insufficient to identify the mechanism.

## Concrete follow-up and source controls

Jiang’s speech adds a specific archival lead to the pilot: 448 dates his instruction to seek a formal Clinton letter to 18 August 1999, the letter to 27 August, and his reply to 1 September. Acquire the letters and drafting instructions before treating the retrospective account as contemporaneous evidence. Page 449 also describes Zhu’s coordination as occurring late at night. That cannot simply be merged into our existing chronology of morning meetings; it may refer to another intervention or compressed recollection. Resolve the event before coding scheduling tactics.

For the next case, **DS379 and its implementation** is a useful bridge between accession and the trade war. Recover submissions, counsel-selection accounts, Chinese and US implementation documents, and subsequent bilateral exchanges. The WTO’s case record already records disagreement over compliance in 2012; that is a lead for reconstructing interaction, not a completed case study. It also corrects the 2018 article’s footnote 471, which gives the wrong case identifier/adoption date for the discussed 2011 report. [WTO DS379 record](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds379_e.htm).

Two further safeguards belong in the research file. Gao’s page 61 places the Appellate Body shutdown in December 2020; the inability to hear new appeals followed the December 2019 loss of a quorum. [Contemporaneous WTO report](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news19_e/dsb_18dec19_e.htm). His interpretation of the unpublished DS516 interim report must remain attributed, and the cited Zhang opening statement could not be retrieved in this pass. Neither becomes a primary-source finding by repetition. Those limitations do not invalidate the readings; they show why even useful scholarship must be checked at the point where it supplies chronology, private motives or a contested legal conclusion.
