# Full-text reading ledger

**8 September 2026.** This pass completes **eight scholarly readings of the versions specified below**, with additional bounded readings and selected primary-source checks. These are eight works, not eight independent evidentiary samples. The 2023 Gao chapter is counted once as a version of the previously screened 2022 article. Shared authorship, reused interviews and repeated documentary sources are recorded in the memos.

Read the [substantive assessment](../first-full-text-synthesis.md) first. This ledger documents its reading base and limits; it is not a claim that the entire literature or the history through 2026 has been covered.

## What remains to establish

The first pass gives a defensible interpretation of available scholarship and several direct documentary findings. It leaves major gaps: the later US trade negotiations, matching Russian and additional Indian cases, the effectiveness of the newer implementation procedures, and much of the Chinese internal approval record. A 2026 publication can examine 1984 or 2012; an observation window must be recorded independently of the date on the cover.

The immediate research sequence is to connect accession to WTO disputes and implementation, then reconstruct the later US trade rounds with comparable evidence. The new Hong Kong study supplies archive leads for a separate major-power baseline check. Missing commercial texts remain useful acquisition leads, but the project proceeds with the online evidence available.

Full source PDFs, OCR, HTML and rendered source pages remain outside Git. The repository contains original analysis, bibliographic metadata, source locators and the audit of what was read. There has been no new outreach, interview or private archive access.
