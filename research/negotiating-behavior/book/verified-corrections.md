# Three factual corrections to the supplied printing

**Verified 15 September 2026.** The foundation reading identified three apparent errors in Solomon’s supplied 2005 printing of the 1999 edition. The printed passages were checked against page images on September 5, so these are not OCR errors. The official records below resolve the previously pending factual checks. They correct names, dates and office attribution; they do not by themselves refute the book’s account of negotiating behavior.

| Printed passage | Correction | Evidence and scope |
|---|---|---|
| p. 18 connects Caleb Cushing’s 1844 mission with the Treaty of Nanking | Cushing and Qiying signed the **Treaty of Wangxia on July 3, 1844**. The Anglo-Chinese Treaty of Nanjing was concluded in 1842. | The [US Capitol’s National Archives artifact description](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/artifact/treaty-wangxia-treaty-peace-amity-and-commerce-between-united-states-and-china-july-3-1844) identifies the treaty, signatories and date. The [Office of the Historian’s historical essay](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1830-1860/china-1) distinguishes the two treaties. These are official retrospective descriptions; the full treaty and its negotiation have not been independently audited here. |
| p. 44 dates completion of normalization to late 1976; p. 23 gives an end-1978 chronology | The announcement was made **December 15, 1978 in Washington**, with diplomatic relations effective **January 1, 1979**. The communiqué separately provided for embassies and ambassadors on March 1. | A contemporary [December 15 backchannel message to Ambassador Unger](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v13/d171), paragraph 2 and enclosed communiqué, specifies the announcement and effective dates. The FRUS source note identifies the Carter Library file. This is stronger than silently choosing one of the book’s two dates. |
| p. 46 associates Geng Biao’s appointment as defense minister with his May 1980 US visit | He visited as **vice premier**; he became defense minister in **March 1981**. His military responsibilities before that appointment remain distinct from the ministerial title. | The [May 28, 1980 Carter–Mondale conversation record](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v13/d310) identifies his office in its subject and participants. The [MFA biography](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/web/ziliao_674904/wjrw_674925/lrfbzjbzzl_674933/200011/t20001107_9881151.shtml) dates his defense appointment to March 1981 and his Central Military Commission secretary-general role to January 1979. The latter is retrospective biography, not the original appointment instrument. |

The correction on Geng matters when reconstructing authority: a military negotiating role, a State Council position and a defense-minister title are not interchangeable. It would still take the relevant instructions to establish what he was empowered to concede. The contemporary meeting record also supplies leads to Brown’s and Brzezinski’s substantive meetings in its second note; those files have not been acquired as part of this correction.
