The [conclusion](../chapters/08-what-survives-solomon.md) has been rebuilt as a substantive synthesis for someone negotiating with China. This is a developmental draft, not a finding of publication readiness. The chapter and this memo are the only files changed for this assignment. ## The argument now made

The opening follows the material results of the 2025 London accommodation: relief reported by software companies and renewed rare-earth magnet shipments, alongside continued Chinese licensing authority. It identifies the negotiator’s choice between useful immediate relief and continuing exposure to future permissions. The account is attributed to the published Chinese framework statement and bounded by the company/customs observations in Chapter 6; it does not reconstruct the unavailable private agreement or imply repeal of the April controls.

The conclusion then develops five connected problems rather than enumerating retained and unresolved propositions. Lamy’s compensation package shows how a retained ceiling can coexist with a valuable concession, provided the intended beneficiary can use the substitute. Accession and the intermediary case distinguish access from usable authority. Financial opening, agricultural approvals and purchases require different judgments of performance. Corporate confidentiality and the Gackle–Cooper hearing disagreement put the acquisition of evidence and the cost of enforcement inside the bargaining problem. WTO, Phase One and climate procedures show different allocations of discretion rather than a national progression toward legalism.

The closing argument brings power into the explanation through particular dependencies and alternatives. China’s processing position made export clearance consequential for foreign production; Russian gas and Indian border cases supply different configurations of interests. The proposed 2026 Board of Trade remains at the examined stage of consultations on coverage and operation. The final sentences return to the foreign government’s choices: accept usable relief while pursuing durable access, reject an announcement that leaves the essential permission untouched, or enforce a failure despite prospective new business.

## What was removed or changed

Removed the concluding narrative’s literal 59-proposition catalog, lengthy account of status methodology, repeated missing-source inventory, and checklist of research questions. The appendix remains linked for exact scope and baseline language. The revised text makes affirmative case-based claims, while retaining uncertainty where it changes the decision being explained.

The update is described through additional institutional routes and bargaining objects: review of American measures, agreed scrutiny, operating permissions and reciprocal access to needed supplies. It does not claim that precise drafting, specialization, failed relationship-building or bargaining after signature were absent from Solomon or Freeman. Freeman’s existing qualifications are acknowledged directly.

No new empirical claim about a national character or Xi-specific effect was introduced. The discussion of centralization is context, not a measured causal explanation. The 2025 sequence is not presented as the invention of export leverage, and the final WTO case does not imply that unchanged appellate arrangements remain available today.

## Requirements expressly preserved

- Solomon’s stronger senior-intervention forecast remains unvalidated at its comparative strength, not refuted by selected failed encounters. The narrative retains the role of detailed drafting; the note makes clear that the later cases do not all reproduce the full conjunction or constitute a representative forecasting test.
- Continued bargaining does not, by itself, establish S-P25’s stronger asymmetry. The initial duty, subsequent demand and expected treatment of both sides are required before calling later accommodation asymmetric.
- Kissinger’s access and Blinken’s acknowledged consultation remain positive evidence. Missing transmission evidence does not prove no influence, and reception is not turned into demonstrated causation of the November result.
- Phase One retains proportionate action, the respondent’s good-faith assessment, prohibited counterresponse under that condition, withdrawal and the absence of an independent adjudicator. A domestic Section 301 investigation is not recast as a confidential Chapter 7 appeal.
- Financial permission predating Phase One is separated from the regulator’s explicit attribution of the accelerated ownership opening. Different purchase benchmarks are not merged. The testimony of affected firms and associations does not establish its effect on policy.
- The 2026 Board’s consultation/design stage is not converted into an operating product schedule. No substantive September endpoint is inferred from the date a docket was checked.
