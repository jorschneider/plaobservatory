The conclusion explains the published distribution of responsibilities and the practical need to carry political support into a subsequent institutional act.

The scholarship is differentiated. Taylor–Garlick offers a rival interpretation based on existing literature, not new original interviews; intervention after failure does not by itself establish prior control. Loh includes original fieldwork, but neither a representative US negotiating sample nor a measured change in concession latitude. Selected primary checks correct source identifications and categorical claims: Party versus state constitution, Patrick Ho's organizational role, the Coast Guard boarding assertion, and the alleged disappearance of South China Sea language from ASEAN statements. Those errors are not used to dismiss unrelated evidence. Neither article becomes a substitute for the instructions missing from May 2019.

The [2023 inquiry](crisis-2023-authority-development.md) supplies a further process distinction. Paired May statements expose disagreement over contact under sanctions; the August MOD statement names sanctions among broader objections. Li's October removal and the announced working-level exchanges precede a November agreement reached while Washington still awaited a named defense minister. The chapter distinguishes a personnel obstacle, authority to restore mechanisms and practical scheduling. It does not infer that dismissal caused restoration or that proposed October talks had already occurred.
