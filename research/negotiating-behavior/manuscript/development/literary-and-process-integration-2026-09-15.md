## What changed

The [independent sequential reading](whole-book-literary-pass-2026-09-15.md) covered all nine narrative bodies at the starting commit. It found that the historical thesis now held together, but interpretation too often interrupted a decision that the evidence had already made intelligible. The root editor applied selected cuts in accession, climate, trade, crisis and Russian boundary passages. The introduction now leaves the outcome of the November 1999 departure threat to Chapter 2, where the full exchange is told. The repeated formal assessment of Solomon's strongest prediction remains principally in Chapter 1 and the conclusion; removing later recaps does not change the proposition assessment.

Practical advice now asks for things an official can seek under uncertainty: a defined decision at a senior meeting, a consistent request through another authorized channel, a list of outstanding permissions and technical tasks, and both delegations' recorded understanding of which draft provisions remain conditional. These suggestions do not promise access to the counterpart's real instructions or a method for reading intent from delay. Their costs and limits remain in the surrounding account.

The 2023 chapter and introduction now ask what gaining access accomplished and what it left unavailable. The manuscript does not claim to have recovered why China's private position on military mechanisms changed between June and November. Xi's knowledge of the balloon mission remains an unresolved source question in the notes. The documented counternarcotics exchange remains separate from the military-channel commitment.

The conclusion compares benefits with different remaining exposures. A released crew can reach home despite the unresolved dispute; an ongoing supply arrangement or local-use permission can still depend on the next authorization. The comparison is an inference from the cases already established, not a new claim about later failure or the confidential price of the London arrangement. It sharpens how a negotiator should value a partial bargain.

## Research that changed the chapter

Two [original 2017 Chinese administrative documents](agricultural-review-process-memo.md) are integrated into Chapter 6. The February notice shifted responsibility for commissioning specified testing from the applicant to the approving authority. The May service guide separated acceptance, testing, laboratory and committee review, internal approval and certificate delivery. Its 270-day period expressly excluded expert review and inspection/testing. The root editor independently inspected the two decisive PDF pages and the relevant substantive notice text before integration.

These documents explain what information would make an application history assessable. They do not establish where Enlist E3 or DP73496 stalled, whether a particular scientific request was necessary, or what diplomatic pressure changed. The older administrative clock is not equated with Phase One's later average, and its exclusions are not silently carried into the agreement. The chapter does not present the 2017 guide as current 2026 law.

The [applicant-literature search](agricultural-applicant-reading-2026-09-15.md) recovered a relevant article abstract and publisher metadata but not the full article or dated regulatory exchange. It remains outside the chapter's causal account and outside the nineteen fully read scholarly works.
