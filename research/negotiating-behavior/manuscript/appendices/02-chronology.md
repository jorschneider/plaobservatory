# Appendix 2. Selective chronology: authority, agreement and performance

*First draft, September 15, 2026. This chronology draws on the manuscript and its source records, supplemented by a bounded direct check of WTO accession dates. It selects fifty-seven consequential sequences across the seven chapters; it is not a complete inventory of meetings or a claim that every case has been examined through September 2026.*

Dates identify the stage supported by the evidence: an encounter, an offer, signature, publication, legal effect or reported implementation. Related stages sometimes share a row, with their dates stated separately. Rows are ordered by the beginning of the selected sequence, so longer intervals overlap. An official or corporate account is identified as such where it establishes reported performance rather than independent verification. “Not established” means that the cited record does not establish the next step; it is not proof that nothing happened.

Chapter notes contain the full citations and reading limitations. Source IDs refer to the registers linked below; FRUS and WTO document numbers are also stable source identifiers. A document's later publication or archival release is not the date on which the underlying bargaining occurred.

## Coverage and source key

The chronology's latest date is not a uniform endpoint for the manuscript. The normalization check concerns December 1978; the claims reconstruction ends at 1977 deferral. The DS379 implementation examination runs through October 2012, with a bounded coda on the sacks domestic proceeding through its April 21, 2016 notice. The climate case ends at December 2015 text adoption. The 2023 crisis case ends with the November agreements and implementation statements. Financial-company evidence is bounded by the examined 2022 accounts and selected January 2023 decision. Biotechnology uses specified certificates and later procedural assessments rather than a census of every application. The export-control reconstruction covers April–July 2025, with July shipment reporting in August; it does not describe all later control changes. The India mechanism check ends May 27–28, 2026; the Board of Trade design account ends July 23; the gas case reaches September 3–4. The September 15 USTR page capture dates retrieval and observed index contents, not a new bilateral agreement.

The table does not rank the cases by success. It makes the object of each claimed success or failure explicit: a changed offer, a signed instrument, a legal entitlement, an administrative permission, a reported withdrawal, an operating business, or a still-unfinished transaction.
