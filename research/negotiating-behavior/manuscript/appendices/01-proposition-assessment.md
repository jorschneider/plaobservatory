# Appendix 1. What remains of the 59 baseline propositions

**Assessment date: 15 September 2026. Complete assessment, revised after the separate proposition audit.**

This appendix preserves all 34 context/Freeman propositions and all 25 process propositions in the unchanged research inventory. Its task is to make every current judgment traceable to a tested component, the strongest relevant support, contrary or limiting evidence, and the remaining uncertainty. The accompanying [assessment data](../proposition-assessment-2026.json) preserve each entire baseline record, including its initial proposed test and original evidence description.

The baseline statements below are the inventory’s exact paraphrases, not quotations claimed to be Solomon’s or Freeman’s exact sentences. Solomon’s principal empirical period is 1967–1984 and his central setting is constructive American–Chinese political negotiation. Freeman’s separate 1999 essay is already an update; his learning claims cannot be treated as original Solomon claims or as independent corroboration of them. Each entry also reproduces its claim-specific baseline qualification, including any original access caveat; the current evidence and linked dossiers control subsequent acquisitions. See the [baseline reading](../../book/context-lessons-freeman.md), [process reading](../../book/process.md) and [unchanged context](../../book/context-claims.json) and [process](../../book/process-claims.json) inventories.

A retained verdict applies only to the **tested component**, not automatically to every mechanism in the full sentence. Evidence that officials cultivate relationships is different from evidence that this changes policy. Technical preparation does not by itself prove superiority; recurring conduct does not prove cultural origin. Supporting observations may be partial or attributed, as stated. “Contrary or limiting” includes counterexamples, competing explanations, reciprocal practices and access limits; it does not label all of them falsifications.

The source IDs link to local dossiers containing the registers, original documents, locators and reading limits. They are not votes: repeated editions, translations, official readouts and reports depending on the same interviews remain related source chains. Cases reused across propositions likewise do not become new independent observations. This appendix consolidates registered readings; it does not claim a fresh full reading of every source or access to undisclosed instructions.

All seven substantive chapters and their stable case dossiers supply this assessment. Dossier citations continue to control the original documents, reading limits and individual case endpoints. Each dossier retains its own event endpoint. The date of this appendix does not claim complete historical coverage through September 2026. The selected corpus cannot estimate general frequencies, identify a uniquely Chinese repertoire, or measure historical change simply by comparing unlike instruments.

Two qualifications govern the whole exercise. First, the unchanged S-P24 extraction says “can signal,” but this softens the printed claim. Solomon p. 149 calls senior intervention “the most reliable sign” of imminent agreement and invokes leaders’ reluctance to risk face; p. 150 asserts a highly consistent endgame. The failed claims/assets example on p. 148 creates a tension with that prediction. The current verdict evaluates this stronger original claim, in its concrete-drafting context, rather than silently substituting a weaker one. Second, S-P25 combines continued bargaining with a stronger asymmetry: demanding the counterpart’s adherence while seeking accommodation oneself. The first is well supported, but does not establish the second.

Three [verified factual corrections](../../book/verified-corrections.md)—the Cushing treaty, normalization date and Geng Biao’s office—remain separate from judgments about behavioral propositions. No entire proposition is classified “correct baseline” on those factual errors alone.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning | Propositions |

| unresolved | Relevant observations or candidate mechanisms exist, but the available evidence does not decide a consequential part of the proposition. | 38 |

The totals describe this assessment’s coverage, not a score for the book. Many entries deliberately retain an observable practice while leaving its proposed cause, efficacy or comparative prevalence unresolved. The [independent assessment review](../proposition-assessment-review-2026-09-15.md) distinguishes a restriction already present in the original from a new finding. No proposition currently receives a revise-or-narrow verdict: missing validation of a frequency or causal mechanism is classified as unresolved. Advice against using rigidity or senior contact as a shortcut is stated separately from a claim of empirical refutation.

## Solomon: context and constraints

### S-C01 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 16–24):** Chinese negotiating style varies with the political purpose of the relationship: officials can use markedly different approaches in confrontation and in constructing cooperation.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The principal study covers a selected setting in which Beijing wanted a positive U.S. relationship. Different behavior after 2018 need not mean the original findings were wrong within that scope. The cited underlying records have not been independently acquired in this workstream.

**Tested component:** Whether differences in negotiating conduct follow the political purpose of the relationship, rather than the issue, channel, personnel or institution.

**Strongest relevant support:** Diplomatic contact reopened while defense access remained unavailable; later specified cooperation coexisted with political disagreement. [C23-09, C23-10, C23-15, C23-17, C23-18](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) This establishes channel-specific variation, not a matched comparison isolating relationship purpose.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Close political relations did not settle successive gas projects. [RUS-G14, RUS-G17, RUS-G19, RUS-G28](../../cases/russia-gas-bargaining.md) Relationship purpose does not determine individual commercial terms.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Keep Solomon’s original restriction to a constructive relationship as a boundary on interpretation. The later cases show variation but do not identify political purpose as its cause; the central comparative claim remains unresolved. Confrontation is not automatic refutation of the cooperation-era baseline.

### S-C02 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 18–22, 25, 31–34):** Chinese domestic social and political traditions help produce a persistent preference for managing negotiation through personal relationships and reciprocal obligations.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Observed use of relationships does not identify culture as its cause. The study assumed distinctive style would be legible to foreign observers; unfamiliarity and observer expectations can influence that assessment.

**Tested component:** Persistence of relationship cultivation; cultural origin separated from its observable use.

**Strongest relevant support:** Xi and Wang publicly honored Kissinger and sought a constructive contribution. [K23-01, K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** No U.S. mission mandate was acknowledged; consultation existed without a specified policy effect. [K23-04, K23-10](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) Neither record identifies cultural causation.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Need comparative decisions linking reciprocal obligations to changed conduct, controlling for policy affinity, reputation, information and access. Persistence is not a test of its cultural explanation.

### S-C03 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 28–38):** Chinese negotiators resist terms or public presentations that depict China as dependent, even when material conditions create a need for foreign cooperation.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter itself records acknowledgment of mutual need and prior acceptance of a subordinate Soviet relationship. National humiliation and imperial status are proposed explanations, not directly observed motives in every episode.

**Tested component:** Resistance to unequal presentation alongside acceptance of qualified material concessions.

**Strongest relevant support:** Liu defended dignity and balanced terms; China later defended a partial agreement with partial tariff relief. [CN-TRADE-LIU201905, CN-TRADE-SCIO20191213](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-chinese-reconstruction.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The accepted text imposed different new burdens, including Chinese IP changes. [POA-EN, POA-CN](../../sources/phase-one-agreement-audit.md) Resistance to dependency language did not require identical obligations.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retained as a public positioning practice. National humiliation as motive and the bargaining price of equal presentation require instructions and draft changes.

### S-C04 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 31–44):** Cultivating a foreign official's investment in access and reputation can create leverage when Chinese counterparts subsequently cast doubt on that relationship.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Personal warmth and repeated demands for commitment are observable; their independent effect on final concessions is not established by the quoted exchanges alone.

**Tested component:** Whether cultivation becomes effective pressure through threatened loss of personal access.

**Strongest relevant support:** Public cultivation and an advocacy request are directly documented. [K23-01, K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) This supplies a possible channel, not the hypothesized threat.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Expected debrief and acknowledged advice do not disclose a threat, responsive concession or modified instruction. [K23-06, K23-10](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Recover the interlocutor’s prior preferences, stake in access, Chinese threat and resulting action. The mechanism has not been demonstrated in the later audit.

### S-C05 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 32–34):** Trusted intermediaries facilitate politically sensitive openings by conveying intentions, supplying information about leaders, and certifying that contact will not produce embarrassment or confrontation.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The mechanism may be universal in secret diplomacy rather than distinctively Chinese. Channels also served American secrecy and political requirements.

**Tested component:** Added information or credibility from a trusted intermediary, distinct from a messenger’s presence.

**Strongest relevant support:** Stern describes confidential specialist exchanges and building trust with Xie. [CLI06](../../cases/climate-2009-2015.md) Retrospective participant account, not a complete paired message chain.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Private status, possible debrief and general advice do not establish a carried operational message or its effect. [K23-04, K23-06, K23-10](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Formal negotiator rapport and a retired intermediary are different channels. No later source packet here demonstrates the complete assurance-to-instruction mechanism or national uniqueness.

### S-C06 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 39–42, 167, 174–175):** Chinese officials exploit existing political or bureaucratic rivalries by favoring counterparts whose preferences better match Chinese objectives and making others fear a loss of influence.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Selecting a receptive channel is not the same as changing a government's preferences. American rivalries are part of the explanation and cannot be coded solely as Chinese tactical success.

**Tested component:** Chinese selection of congenial counterparts versus exploitation of bureaucratic rivalry.

**Strongest relevant support:** Senior officials welcomed a sympathetic former official; Wang contrasted current policy with Nixon/Kissinger diplomacy. [K23-01, K23-02, K23-03](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** U.S. agricultural witnesses disagreed about new enforcement action. [POI12](../../cases/phase-one-implementation-2020-2026.md) No evidence Beijing created or successfully exploited that division.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Need contemporaneous selection strategy, rival actors’ reactions and an altered balance of policy. Rivalry and preferred access alone do not demonstrate leverage.

### S-C07 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 42–44):** Uncertainty over meetings with senior Chinese leaders can be used to induce foreign counterparts to clarify intentions or accommodate Chinese conditions.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon expressly speculates that the second Nixon meeting was canceled over Taiwan; illness was the Chinese explanation. That case cannot be counted as proven tactical manipulation.

**Tested component:** Conditional access as a bargaining object, distinguished from uncertainty over a senior appointment.

**Strongest relevant support:** Chinese statements connected denied defense contact to political conditions. [C23-06, C23-11, C23-12](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Blinken met Xi but announced no immediate military-channel agreement. [C23-15, C23-16](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) A granted senior meeting was not sufficient for the requested result.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** This supports selective access as observable conduct, not the specific mechanism that uncertain scheduling induced accommodation. Need appointment communications and recipient responses.

### S-C08 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 44–48, 172):** Detailed preparation and coordinated management of information, protocol, and timing give Chinese negotiators an operational advantage when counterparts are less prepared.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Preparation quality is not proof of a superior outcome. The p46 Geng Biao appointment narrative is present in the original scan but appears historically problematic and should not be reproduced without external verification.

**Tested component:** Preparation, information circulation, authority to challenge assessments and resulting advantage.

**Strongest relevant support:** Detailed Chinese submissions and benchmark proposals demonstrate preparation and some accepted technical argument. [DS379-03, DS379-04](../../cases/wto-ds379-legal-process.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Jost proposes a failure of institutional information handling during EP-3. [EP319](../../cases/ep3-2001.md) Selected retrospective scholarship; contemporaneous Chinese deliberations and parts of its source chain remain unverified.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No comparative estimate of operational advantage. Correcting the Geng Biao anecdote in the baseline does not by itself refute preparation as a mechanism; see the separate verified-corrections dossier.

### S-C09 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 48–50):** Chinese negotiators shape their statements for superiors and political rivals as well as for the foreign counterpart, encouraging disciplined repetition and reluctance to reveal unapproved positions.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Repeated language suggests instructions but does not expose their contents. Foreign observers' impressions of internal audiences require corroboration.

**Tested component:** Whether official repetition reflects a domestic audience and withheld authorization.

**Strongest relevant support:** Sector participant describes requests moving through regulator and central approval hierarchies. [EWT-13](../../sources/wto-financial-participant-note.md) Participant reconstruction with noted chronology/institution issues.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Published chronology describes unsuccessful coordination and a collectively defended limit. [EWT-01](../../cases/eu-wto-2000-comparison.md) The same refusal can reflect an agreed substantive ceiling, not political rivalry.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Obtain instructions, reporting drafts and audience reactions. Repetition is observed; the domestic incentive causing it is not independently identified.

### S-C10 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 49–50, 168, 170):** In high-level political negotiations, flexibility often requires senior authorization after the foreign position has been tested; senior intervention can release an apparent deadlock.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** A leader's visible intervention does not prove that lack of prior authorization caused delay. Issue stakes, tactical theater, and substantive incompatibility remain alternatives.

**Tested component:** Identifiable senior or regulatory decisions permitting or confirming particular negotiating terms, with frequency and the prior-testing sequence assessed separately.

**Strongest relevant support:** Deng and Washington made bounded decisions while specific disagreement remained. [NOR-166, NOR-167, NOR-170, NOR-172](../../book/normalization-1978-primary-check.md) Participant identifies fresh State Council and regulator authorization for financial provisions. [EWT-13](../../sources/wto-financial-participant-note.md) Authority chain remains retrospective on the Chinese side.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Zhu’s March 2000 meeting did not close the agreement; May confirmation concerned a limit as well as compensation. [EWT-01](../../cases/eu-wto-2000-comparison.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain the observed authorization component in these episodes. The claim that flexibility often arrives only after fully testing the counterpart, prior lack of instructions as the cause of delay, leader persuasion and general success rates remain unresolved. Senior confirmation can concern a ceiling as well as a concession.

### S-C11 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 50–56):** Leadership weakness, interministerial disagreement, and problems obtaining local compliance can immobilize negotiation while the public Chinese position continues to appear unified.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon marks the Lin Biao, radical-faction, and Zhou explanations as obscure, inferred, likely, or circumstantial. Unified public speech does not establish unity or conflict by itself.

**Tested component:** Specific institutional blockage, rather than inferring division from a unified public stance or delay.

**Strongest relevant support:** Solomon’s contemporary analysis links the failed claims arrangement to internal pressures; the later U.S. review records withdrawal. [CLA06, CLA10](../../book/claims-assets-1973-primary-check.md) Partly redacted source chain; memorandum and book are not independent witnesses.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Long canola approval histories exist in China and the EU. [AG05, AG16](../../cases/agricultural-approvals-implementation.md) Elapsed time does not identify Chinese leadership weakness or interministerial conflict.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No contemporary Chinese veto record supplies the decisive mechanism in these cases. Distinguish capacity, substantive review, applicant responses and agreed priorities from factional conflict.

### S-C12 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 44–45, 167–170):** The shift from a small leadership-managed opening to restored bureaucracies and technical issues increased the number of relevant Chinese institutions and could slow decisionmaking.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon already recognizes both centralization and bureaucratic friction. The apparent late-1976 normalization date on p44 is printed in the scan but conflicts with his own p23 chronology and is not used here.

**Tested component:** Multiplicity of institutions and sequential permissions, not an inferred decline in central control.

**Strongest relevant support:** Establishment, licensing, shareholder registration and branch permissions are distinct decisions. [FIN-07, FIN-16, FIN-26](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md) WMCC has specified functions alongside the Special Representatives mechanism. [IND-MEA-17963](../../cases/india-border-2020-2024.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The same institutional system implemented an accelerated ownership timetable and operating business. [FIN-15, FIN-26](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md) Multiple stages do not establish that fragmentation caused delay.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The historical expansion mechanism was already in Solomon. Comparative timing and coordination effects remain unmeasured; the baseline’s normalization-date error is separately corrected.

### S-C13 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 4–5, 170, 173):** Chinese political negotiators tend to prefer broad understandings and principles to exhaustive legal detail, but can set principles aside or rationalize concrete compromise when agreement serves their interests.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The study's high-political baseline is not a representative sample of commercial or technical negotiations. A principle that can always be overridden risks becoming an unfalsifiable explanation.

**Original-text qualification:** Solomon p. 5 already permits a negotiator seeking agreement to set aside early principles and reach a concrete accord; pp. 170 and 173 discuss political relationships and American–Chinese differences in approach. A later legal or technical instrument is not by itself a counterexample to that qualified political preference.

**Tested component:** A relative preference for broad principles within political negotiation, together with the acknowledged ability to reach concrete compromise.

**Strongest relevant support:** Chinese public diplomacy continues to frame demands through broad relationship principles. [C23-14, C23-16](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) Use of principles does not identify preference over an available, more specific alternative or its effect on the bargain.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** China advanced detailed legal arguments and accepted a precise procedural bargain. [DS379-03, DS379-07](../../cases/wto-ds379-legal-process.md) Litigation and an implementation-procedure agreement concern different institutional choices from the political understandings at the center of the original claim. Specific obligations coexist with political discretion. [POA-EN, POA-CN](../../sources/phase-one-agreement-audit.md) The existence of specific provisions is compatible with the original allowance for concrete compromise.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The original scope and exceptions must be preserved, not counted as revisions discovered by this study. Public principle language and detailed later instruments coexist, but do not establish the relative preference, its constraints or a historical change. Those questions require comparable choices over document form, drafts and the terms negotiators rejected.

### S-C14 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 6, 8, 172–173):** Chinese continuity and command of the negotiating record can create leverage over American officials whose turnover and dispersed archives impair knowledge of earlier commitments.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Claims of complete Chinese command should not be accepted as literal universals. Record knowledge, the legitimacy of an interpretation, and the ability to obtain concessions are separate variables.

**Tested component:** Knowledge and use of the prior record, separated from superior memory and concession leverage.

**Strongest relevant support:** Liu invoked an asserted Argentina purchase consensus. [CN-TRADE-LIU201905](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-chinese-reconstruction.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The contemporaneous U.S. dinner readout explicitly left the amount unagreed. [TUS05](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-us-reconstruction.md) This discrepancy does not determine which private understanding existed.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No comparative personnel/archive measure establishes Chinese memory superiority or resulting leverage. Both accurate invocation and favorable reconstruction must be checked against the common text.

### S-C15 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 9, 173):** Placing a technical issue within a broader relationship framework can make compromise easier for Chinese decisionmakers to justify.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The formulation may accompany a prior decision rather than cause it. Advice is not itself a tested intervention.

**Tested component:** Whether relationship framing enabled a particular technical compromise.

**Strongest relevant support:** Opening was presented through bilateral benefit and domestic reform. [FIN-01, FIN-14](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md) Leaders endorsed nationally differentiated climate intentions and cooperation. [CLI12, CLI15](../../cases/climate-2009-2015.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Domestic reform and timetable changes preceded the final bilateral agreement. [FIN-03, FIN-10, FIN-11](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Framing can justify a prior decision as well as cause a new one. Need proposals and decision records showing an otherwise unacceptable technical term became acceptable through the broader frame.

### S-C16 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 9, 165, 174):** Deadlines create bargaining vulnerability for whichever side most needs an agreement; Chinese negotiators try to exploit American urgency but are themselves vulnerable when Chinese deadlines bind.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Civilizational time horizons cannot substitute for documented incentives. Delay may also reflect authorization or uncertainty rather than tactical patience.

**Tested component:** Reciprocal use and adjustment of deadlines; not a universal Chinese time advantage.

**Strongest relevant support:** November 12 and 14 American letters used departure threats and an extension linked to a Zhu meeting. [CN-WTO-B3](../../cases/wto-1999-chinese-reconstruction.md) B3 pp.1067–1070 and 1094; effectiveness disputed.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** American tariff deadlines were repeatedly extended. [TUS09](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-us-reconstruction.md) Regulatory duration includes non-Chinese as well as Chinese constraints. [AG16, AG05](../../cases/agricultural-approvals-implementation.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain the conditional proposition that urgency can matter. Chinese exploitation of a particular deadline and measured concession effects remain unresolved.

### S-C17 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 9, 168–169, 175):** Confidential exchanges can reduce public expectation pressures and increase perceived seriousness, while publicity can be used to constrain a counterpart's room to compromise.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The claimed benefits of confidentiality may be selection effects: promising negotiations may be easier to keep quiet. Public constraints can also supply leverage or credibility.

**Tested component:** Documented management of different public audiences, with causal benefits left unproved.

**Strongest relevant support:** The released letter and U.S. legal guidance distinguish immediate settlement language from preserved responsibility positions. [EP303, EP321](../../cases/ep3-2001.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Contemporary and later American accounts disagree over the purpose and effects of publishing accession commitments. [US-APR08-BRIEFING](../../cases/wto-1999-us-reconstruction.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain as an observed instrument of bargaining. Confidentiality’s independent contribution, audience constraint and publication effects require private decisions and reactions.

### S-C18 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 157–160, 166, 176–177):** Competent management of negotiating tactics may improve control and mutual respect, but its effects on agreement outcomes are often secondary to state interests and official discipline; excessive manipulation can undermine trust.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Chapter 4 develops recommendations rather than testing competing strategies. Its proposed mechanisms should not be reported as demonstrated outcome effects.

**Tested component:** State interests, institutional obligations and available options as necessary controls on tactical explanation.

**Strongest relevant support:** Political warmth coexists with different commercial closure stages. [RUS-G03, RUS-G17, RUS-G28](../../cases/russia-gas-bargaining.md) A negotiated timetable changed domestic regulation and produced usable access. [FIN-15, FIN-26](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Blinken acknowledged seeking Kissinger’s advice. [K23-10](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) Material-interest explanations should not erase possible interpersonal contributions.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain the anti-omnipotence qualification; the comparative magnitude of tactical and substantive effects has not been estimated, and counterproductive manipulation is not separately tested.

### S-C19 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 175–176):** Rigidity or polemical conduct by Chinese negotiators is usually a sign of leadership conflict, making accommodation less likely.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** This is stronger than the underlying evidence warrants without testing: chapter 2 itself lists several other causes of inflexibility. Public language cannot reliably identify leadership conflict on its own.

**Tested component:** Whether observed rigidity usually indicates leadership conflict and predicts reduced capacity for accommodation.

**Strongest relevant support:** A contemporary participant-analyst proposed internal pressure as a cause. [CLA06](../../book/claims-assets-1973-primary-check.md) Its underlying evidence is partly inaccessible and is reused by the book.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Firm positions accompany specific collective ceilings and limits in the European mandate. [EWT-01](../../cases/eu-wto-2000-comparison.md) The record does not establish the presence or absence of Chinese leadership conflict behind the collective ceiling. Institutional review and non-Chinese delay provide alternatives to a factional diagnosis. [AG09, AG16](../../cases/agricultural-approvals-implementation.md) These are alternative mechanisms in different regulatory settings, not a frequency test of rigidity in high political negotiation.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The empirical frequency claim remains unresolved: no matched sample or sufficient Chinese internal evidence establishes how often rigidity reflects leadership conflict. As practical advice, do not diagnose such conflict from public rigidity alone; investigate authority, substantive limits and competing explanations. That evidentiary caution is not a demonstrated revision of behavior or a finding that leadership conflict was absent.

### S-C20 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 162–170):** American turnover, divided authority, and relatively open information flows can both create openings for Chinese pressure and frustrate Chinese efforts to secure stable commitments and personal access.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** National-character contrasts in Table 4 are hypotheses, not measured attributes. Chinese perceptions of American institutions are often inferred rather than documented internally.

**Tested component:** Interactive effects of American divided authority and changing public positions.

**Strongest relevant support:** Executive disagreement and separate congressional PNTR authority are explicit. [US-APR08-BRIEFING, US-NOV15-BERGER](../../cases/wto-1999-us-reconstruction.md) Industry witnesses favor different enforcement choices. [POI12](../../cases/phase-one-implementation-2020-2026.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** A long-retired official remained available for consultation despite turnover. [K23-10](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) Personal continuity can coexist with institutional dispersion.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain institutional constraints as part of the bilateral explanation. Chinese perceptions, deliberate exploitation and the net advantage of openness remain unproved.

## Freeman: the late-1990s update

### F01 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 182–184):** By the late 1990s, Chinese negotiators still emphasized long-term relationships and shared principles, using deferral of difficult issues to allow common interests and trust to develop.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Freeman does not disclose a new systematic archive or interview sample. Brief case mentions do not establish causal mechanisms, and endorsement of Solomon is not independent proof.

**Original-text qualification:** Freeman p. 183 presents the trust-building rationale as the Chinese view, then explicitly allows success in an appropriate context, failure and, continuing on p. 184, uncertain success. He does not claim that time or relationship language necessarily resolves disagreement.

**Tested component:** Deferral and relationship cultivation are observable; the intended and actual growth of common interests or trust requires a separate causal test.

**Strongest relevant support:** Reserved areas persisted across cooperation and were later delimited. [RUS-T1991-CN, RUS-T2001, RUS-T2004](../../cases/russia-border-settlement.md) The sequence establishes reservation followed by later settlement, not the intention behind deferral or the mechanism producing settlement. Historical relationship recognition persisted in 2023. [K23-01, K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) Recognition of an old relationship does not establish a deferred bargain or increased mutual trust.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Repeated political endorsement did not eliminate commercial disagreement. [RUS-G14, RUS-G17, RUS-G28](../../cases/russia-gas-bargaining.md) Continued disagreement is compatible with Freeman’s explicit allowance for failure or uncertain success; it does not revise that qualification.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The repertoire recurs, but its intended and actual causal contribution remains unresolved. Do not convert Freeman’s conditional and partly attributed account into an automatic trust-producing rule and then claim to refute it. The Russia sequence requires intervening decisions and alternatives; the Kissinger case requires a specified transmission and policy effect.

### F02 — untested

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 184–185):** Foreign-exchange and official spending restrictions materially reinforced the Chinese preference for negotiating in China.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The anecdotes are not accompanied by the administrative records here. Budget constraints can explain some venue choices without exhausting cultural, intelligence, or strategic reasons.

**Tested component:** Budget and foreign-exchange rules as a cause of negotiating venue.

**Strongest relevant support:** No adequate case-level test in the acquired corpus; no evidence is counted as confirmation or refutation.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** No adequate case-level test in the acquired corpus; no evidence is counted as confirmation or refutation.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No dated travel-budget or entertainment-rule comparison was acquired. Beijing meetings cannot test this economic explanation, and later overseas meetings alone cannot establish why venue changed.

### F03 — untested

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 185–186):** Having foreign counterparts state their positions first gives Chinese diplomats a politically safer record from which to seek revised instructions, reducing the risk of appearing to advocate foreign interests.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Speaking second also has universal informational bargaining advantages. Freeman's mechanism must be distinguished from tactical extraction rather than replacing it by assertion.

**Tested component:** Speaking second as protection against appearing to advocate foreign interests when seeking new instructions.

**Strongest relevant support:** No adequate case-level test in the acquired corpus; no evidence is counted as confirmation or refutation.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** No adequate case-level test in the acquired corpus; no evidence is counted as confirmation or refutation.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Authority accounts do not reconstruct speaking order, internal reporting or career risk. Need paired meeting and internal submission records; ordinary information-gathering is a competing mechanism.

### F04 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 186–187):** Reluctance to delegate and the need for rapid authoritative guidance reinforce negotiation near Chinese decisionmakers, even after communications technology improves.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Home-ground intelligence and protocol advantages are additional causes. Faster communication could reduce delay while increasing central micromanagement, so technical improvement alone does not predict greater delegate autonomy.

**Tested component:** Proximity to authority, communications capacity and delegation as determinants of venue.

**Strongest relevant support:** Financial negotiator describes referrals to State Council and regulator leaders. [EWT-13](../../sources/wto-financial-participant-note.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The strategic channel operated in Vienna, away from Beijing. [C23-09, C23-10](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) A foreign venue does not identify how its mandate was supplied.

## Solomon: process and tactics

### S-P01 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 57–58, 75–76, 147–151):** PRC political negotiations commonly progress from relationship and agenda construction, through extended assessment, to a brief agreement-making phase and subsequent implementation bargaining.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter selects a few major agreements and repeatedly reuses them. The endgame admits abortion or continued bargaining. Cultural and institutional interpretations are not independently established by the regularity of a reconstructed sequence. Do not count repeated references to one episode as independent cases.

**Original-text qualification:** The table on Solomon p. 58 expressly allows concluding an agreement, reserving a position or aborting negotiation; p. 147 likewise describes alternatives to settlement. Failure to finish is therefore not by itself contrary evidence to his process model.

**Tested component:** Typical order, overlap and duration of the proposed stages within comparable political negotiations, including failed and suspended efforts.

**Strongest relevant support:** An announced but incomplete October package became a signed January instrument. [TUS12, TUS15](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-us-reconstruction.md) A sequence from incomplete announcement to signature does not measure the preceding stages or establish their typical duration. Implementation generated further senior exchanges. [POI01, POI03, POI04](../../cases/phase-one-implementation-2020-2026.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Different channels moved at different speeds within the same relationship. [C23-09, C23-15, C23-17](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) Different channels within one bilateral relationship are not necessarily stages of the same negotiation. Senior movement did not produce closure in the reconstructed claims sequence. [CLA04, CLA05, CLA10](../../book/claims-assets-1973-primary-check.md) The original model already allows unsuccessful endings; this case helps prevent selection only on signed agreements.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Stage order and typical duration remain unresolved. Define a bargaining object and its entry and exit points before assigning stages; include interrupted, failed and repeated sequences. The model remains a possible descriptive organizer, but neither selected successful chronologies nor failures already admitted by the original decide its empirical regularity.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Observed access is retained. Deliberate exclusion to influence internal U.S. competition and more favorable terms remain unestablished; do not turn retired advisory access into authority.

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 71–75, 128, 150–151):** PRC political negotiators favor broad principles and flexible political documents, subsequently invoking those formulations to constrain the counterpart while retaining discretion over concrete arrangements.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon concedes agreements apparently inconsistent with early principles and American refusals to accept Chinese formulations. **Baseline claim (printed pp. 110–111, 139–140, 153–156):** Negotiating tone varies with individuals, assigned roles, senior instructions, and domestic circumstances; alternating harder and more accommodating presentations can affect expectations about compromise.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter calls the hard–soft tactic universal and notes that Deng could be abrasive while Huang could be conciliatory. The portraits are American-facing and partly impressionistic. Unresolved Mao–Zhou politics limit certainty about who directed every presentation.

**Tested component:** Variation in tone across individuals, instructions and roles, and its effect on expectations.

**Strongest relevant support:** Conrad’s interviews describe a shift from climate specialists to the premier’s foreign-policy team. [CLI05](../../cases/climate-2009-2015.md) Retrospective explanatory account without released mandates.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Warm personal presentation coexisted with firm substantive demands. [K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No sufficiently paired same-person or assigned-role comparison establishes orchestrated hard–soft tactics or a resulting concession. Tone and proposal changes must be coded separately.

### S-P17 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 113–122):** PRC officials use public communication around negotiations to establish agendas, raise or reduce expectations, advertise limits, and suggest openings, with different outlets offering different degrees of commitment.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon documents learning about television, failed expectation-raising, and respect for some confidentiality requests. The historical media hierarchy is not automatically valid later. His suggestion that a Chinese press criticism influenced the Mayaguez response is conjectural and needs independent causal evidence.

**Tested component:** Public communication used to characterize the agenda, prior obligations and permissible movement.

**Strongest relevant support:** White papers and briefing defended different stages of the negotiation. [CN-TRADE-WP2018, CN-TRADE-WP2019, CN-TRADE-SCIO20191213](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-chinese-reconstruction.md) English and Chinese presentations supported immediate crew return while responsibility remained disputed. [EP303, EP304, EP305](../../cases/ep3-2001.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The U.S. publication dispute shows counterpart publicity and disputed effects too. [US-APR08-BRIEFING](../../cases/wto-1999-us-reconstruction.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain observable public positioning. A contemporary outlet hierarchy, internal dissemination and causal effects on public pressure need direct evidence rather than projection of Solomon’s older media categories.

### S-P18 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 122–125):** Rapid collection and internal circulation of foreign reporting can inform PRC bargaining while also generating domestic reactions that constrain negotiators or intensify later responses.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon says the selection procedures for internal publications were unknown. Some feedback mechanisms rely on private explanations or chronological inference. Abundant access to information does not prove unfiltered reporting to leaders or correct interpretation of a foreign political system.

**Tested component:** Internal circulation and selection of foreign reporting causing a changed bargaining response.

**Strongest relevant support:** Jost’s institutional account offers an information-flow mechanism. [EP319](../../cases/ep3-2001.md) Selected scholarship and source-chain limits do not replace Chinese circulation records.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** American assessments of Xi’s possible ignorance are not direct Chinese evidence. [C23-20, C23-21](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) Later reports partly repeat the same assessment chain.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** No acquired Chinese internal report and subsequent decision establish the feedback loop. Information availability, accuracy, transmission and political constraint remain separate variables.

### S-P19 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 105–106, 125–129, 140–143):** PRC negotiators preserve and invoke the prior record, including protests and predecessors' words, but may restate disputed commitments in forms more favorable to their current position.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter's translation and distortion allegations require checking against originals. Cultural claims about written Chinese do not independently explain document management. Table 2 is a set of contextual signaling hypotheses, not an automatic dictionary of intent. Solomon also acknowledges mutual overreading of symbols.

**Tested component:** Invocation of prior commitments; favorable restatement and distortion remain separate tests.

**Strongest relevant support:** Liu invoked an Argentina numerical understanding. [CN-TRADE-LIU201905](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-chinese-reconstruction.md) Both sides used prior understandings while emphasizing different relationship obligations. [IND-MEA-6534, IND-CN-2022-SUN, IND-MEA-35739](../../cases/india-border-2020-2024.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** The U.S. dinner readout left purchases unquantified. [TUS05](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-us-reconstruction.md) Records distinguish common principles from unilateral positions and non-endorsement. [NOR-168, NOR-172](../../book/normalization-1978-primary-check.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain invocation as interactive conduct. A discrepancy is not automatically strategic distortion; recover original agreed wording, reservations, translations and subsequent claims.

### S-P20 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 130–131, 134–135):** Statements of independence, indifference, or foreign indebtedness can portray the counterpart as the party needing agreement and responsible for repairing the relationship.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon interprets these claims through the relationship game and anxiety about dependence, but real asymmetries or genuine performance disputes may also explain them. Analyst disbelief is not a reliable measure of the speaker's actual valuation.

**Tested component:** Publicly assigning repair responsibility and portraying Chinese cooperation as conditional on American conduct.

**Strongest relevant support:** Chinese officials called for corrective U.S. action and appropriate conditions for contact. [C23-06, C23-11, C23-14](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Chinese leaders also requested constructive advocacy and mutual improvement. [K23-01, K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md) These are compatible with valuing the relationship, not proof of indifference.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain the public rhetorical practice. Actual private valuation, anxiety about dependence and whether the portrayal extracted concessions are unresolved.

### S-P21 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 131–134, 138–140):** PRC officials use challenges to the counterpart's resolve, logic, motives, and policy consistency to contest its rationale and encourage behavior favorable to Chinese interests.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Kissinger sometimes rejected the premise explicitly, and Deng's debunking could resist perceived American pressure. The chapter's linkage to dialectical reasoning is interpretive; similar forms of persuasion are internationally familiar and may express sincere substantive disagreement.

**Tested component:** Challenges to policy consistency and motives as visible argumentation.

**Strongest relevant support:** China accused Washington of changing positions and making unacceptable demands. [CN-TRADE-WP2018, CN-TRADE-WP2019](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-chinese-reconstruction.md) Wang challenged the strategic logic of containing China. [K23-02](../../reporting/kissinger-2023-intermediary-audit.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Blinken maintained U.S. national-security restrictions and Taiwan policy despite Chinese demands. [C23-15](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The persuasion attempt is observed, not its success or derivation from distinctive dialectical reasoning. Sincere disagreement and familiar bargaining argument remain alternatives.

### S-P22 — retain within tested scope

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 135–137):** Openly specifying differences can coexist with limited cooperation, reassuring domestic and allied audiences while clarifying the common interests that sustain an agreement.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Solomon distinguishes the domestic and strategic conditions of different episodes and credits compromise by both parties. The explanation does not imply that all harsh rhetoric is compatible with agreement or that disagreement is merely theatrical.

**Tested component:** Explicit disagreement coexisting with a specified limited bargain.

**Strongest relevant support:** Both governments accepted normalization with the arms-sales disagreement preserved. [NOR-170, NOR-172](../../book/normalization-1978-primary-check.md) Shared dialogue mechanisms coexisted with Taiwan and technology disputes. [C23-17, C23-18](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) Different national responsibilities and common procedures coexist. [CLI15, CLI17](../../cases/climate-2009-2015.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Immediate practical resolution did not settle responsibility or future reconnaissance. [EP303, EP306](../../cases/ep3-2001.md) Limited cooperation does not imply the remaining disagreement is theatrical or harmless.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Retain coexistence. Domestic/allied reassurance as the cause of explicit disagreement and the conditions under which rhetoric prevents agreement remain unmeasured.

### S-P23 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 143–147):** Control over the schedule and apparent willingness to wait can help PRC negotiators exploit the counterpart's deadline, but Chinese negotiators are also susceptible to time pressure.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** Kissinger described both sides using the 1972 deadline. The United States declined an unfavorable deal in February 1982, and Solomon recognizes initial British time leverage over Hong Kong. Cultural reserve does not establish an inherent Chinese advantage in patience.

**Tested component:** Chinese schedule control producing favorable terms, with reciprocal vulnerability retained.

**Strongest relevant support:** Long-running talks persisted despite Russian near-completion claims. [RUS-G14, RUS-G17, RUS-G28](../../cases/russia-gas-bargaining.md) Optimistic targets are not proven binding deadlines or evidence of exploitation.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** U.S. departure letters actively managed the deadline. [CN-WTO-B3](../../cases/wto-1999-chinese-reconstruction.md) Washington controlled and extended tariff dates. [TUS09](../../cases/us-trade-2018-2020-us-reconstruction.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Direct evidence supports reciprocal timing tactics more than a measured Chinese patience advantage. Need private deadlines, alternative offers and a concession sequence showing their effect.

### S-P24 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 147–151):** A shift toward concrete drafting and intervention by a senior PRC leader can signal that agreement is authorized and near, even after apparent deadlock.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter's face-based explanation is not independently established, and selected successful cases cannot determine reliability. Senior authority does not eliminate bureaucratic reversal: the chapter cites repudiation of the 1973 claims/assets solution. Several local date inconsistencies require original-source checking.

**Original-text qualification:** The unchanged S-P24 extraction says “can signal,” but this softens the printed claim. Solomon p. 149 calls senior intervention “the most reliable sign” of imminent agreement and invokes leaders’ reluctance to risk face; p. 150 asserts a highly consistent endgame. The failed claims/assets example on p. 148 creates a tension with that prediction. The current verdict evaluates this stronger original claim, in its concrete-drafting context, rather than silently substituting a weaker one.

**Tested component:** The original ranking of senior intervention as the most reliable sign of approaching agreement, considered with concrete drafting and the proposed face mechanism.

**Strongest relevant support:** Concrete text and Deng’s intervention preceded successful normalization. [NOR-159, NOR-166, NOR-170](../../book/normalization-1978-primary-check.md) A Russian authorization attached drafts and bounded discretion immediately before signature. [RUS-AUTH2004, RUS-T2004](../../cases/russia-border-settlement.md) This is a counterpart authority record, not Chinese mandate evidence or proof outsiders had it prospectively.

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Zhou’s movement did not close the claims bargain and the monetary offer was later withdrawn. [CLA04, CLA05, CLA10](../../book/claims-assets-1973-primary-check.md) Solomon already acknowledged this failure; the new reconstruction sharpens its unfinished terms rather than discovering failure as a new possibility. Zhu’s March meeting did not close accession talks. [EWT-01](../../cases/eu-wto-2000-comparison.md) This defeats a deterministic shortcut from a senior meeting to closure, but does not rank the predictive value of all available signals. Xi received Blinken without military-channel agreement. [C23-15, C23-16](../../cases/us-china-crisis-2023.md) The record does not show the full concrete-drafting condition for the military-channel request or a promise by Xi that it would be settled.

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** The comparative ranking and frequency remain unresolved: selected successes and failures cannot establish whether senior intervention is the most reliable available sign. As practical advice, identify what a leader authorizes, confirms or leaves unsettled instead of equating contact with imminent closure. That revision in use is distinct from empirical refutation. Missing later drafts prevent a full test of the conjunction, and the face mechanism is unproved.

### S-P25 — unresolved

**Baseline claim (printed pp. 151–153):** Implementation can remain a bargaining arena in which PRC officials demand adherence from the counterpart while seeking adjustment, accommodation, or new concessions themselves.

**Baseline scope and qualification:** The chapter mixes formal agreements with contemplated visits and expressions of intent; these are not equivalent commitments. Deng's May 1978 acknowledgment that a new administration could reconsider qualifies the claim of rigid continuity. Commercial complaints require independent evidence before generalization to political agreements.

**Tested component:** Two components kept distinct: implementation bargaining, and asymmetric Chinese demands for adherence while seeking accommodation itself.

**Strongest relevant support:** China defended fulfillment, pressed U.S. obligations and restrictions, and carried issues into later bargains. [POI03, POI04, POI07, POI14, POI17](../../cases/phase-one-implementation-2020-2026.md) Closest evidence of reciprocal demands; private reservations and formal appeals are incomplete. The parties negotiated procedures for further implementation disagreement. [DS379-07](../../cases/wto-ds379-legal-process.md)

**Strongest contrary or limiting evidence:** Negotiated access produced a regulatory acceleration and an operating U.S.-owned firm. [FIN-15, FIN-26, FIN-28](../../cases/financial-access-implementation.md) Consensual arrangements made boundary implementation workable. [RUS-USE1999, RUS-MEMO2004](../../cases/russia-border-settlement.md) Later aircraft work concerned issues expressly deferred, not necessarily reopened obligations. [EP303, EP306](../../cases/ep3-2001.md)

**Assessment and unresolved remainder:** Continuing bargaining is strongly observed, but that alone cannot retain the full proposition. Need obligation-specific Chinese demands, its own requested departures, comparable counterpart conduct and agreed exceptions to establish asymmetry rather than ordinary implementation, mutual adjustment or disputed compliance.
