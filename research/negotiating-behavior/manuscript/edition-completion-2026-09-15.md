## What remains uncertain

No new interviews, correspondence or access to private Chinese archives were undertaken. Missing drafts, mandates, application histories and intermediary transmissions limit particular causal explanations. The case design does not estimate a uniquely Chinese national style, an average Xi effect or general predictive reliability. These are stated research limits, not unfinished chapters disguised as completed work. The user’s instruction to do the best possible work with available online texts governs this edition’s scope.
