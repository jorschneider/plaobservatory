# Editorial and evidentiary audit: from research dossier to book

**15 September 2026.** This audit examines the repository's actual book notes, 59 proposition records, five comparative syntheses, case reconstructions and reading ledger. It assesses the argument and coverage, not the amount of material collected. Source claims below refer to the existing dossier; this audit conducted no new external research and does not independently validate every cited document. It precedes review of any new manuscript chapters.

## The book we can defend—and the work still needed

The dossier already makes a substantive correction to common accounts of Chinese bargaining: a political announcement, a provisional bargain, a signed obligation, an implementing permission and a commercially useful result can describe different things. Its best original contributions recover those distinctions from documents. The November 1999 insurance offers, the financial opening timetable and company disclosures, the four 2017 biotechnology certificates, and the Russian authorization and joint-use instruments provide unusually concrete material. These are the foundation of an argument, not merely methodological preliminaries.

The dossier does **not yet demonstrate a general trajectory of Chinese negotiating behavior from the 1980s to 2026**. Its principal later American sequence jumps from accession in 1999 to economic bargaining in 2017–2026. Institutional and legal scholarship partially bridges that interval, but the corresponding negotiations have not been reconstructed at the case level. Military crisis negotiations with the United States are still absent. The corpus is strongest on the content and implementation of commitments, weaker on the interpersonal behavior prominent in Solomon's subtitle, and weakest on the causal effect of either behavior or institutional change.

The manuscript should make a positive argument around **how political authority is translated into commitments that the counterpart can use**. It should distinguish two questions throughout: what capabilities and instruments changed over time, and what behavior changes when the issue, relationship purpose or counterpart changes. These are connected comparisons, not interchangeable explanations. The available material supports a thesis of enduring political authorization operating through a more extensive set of specialized instruments. It has not yet established that specialization systematically improves flexibility, speed or compliance.

This is an original analytical monograph, not an authorized new edition and not a claim to reproduce Solomon's confidential access. A workable planning scope is seven substantive chapters, an introduction and a conclusion, roughly 55,000–70,000 words before notes. That is a limit on sprawl, not a quality target. Each chapter must advance the argument through reconstructed choices, rather than become another literature inventory. Supporting registers remain an accompanying research apparatus.

## What must not be presented as the new discovery

The [baseline audit](../book/context-lessons-freeman.md) makes four tempting narratives untenable.

1. **“Chinese behavior changed because interests matter more than culture.”** Solomon already gives interests and official discipline priority over the effects of tactics, and distinguishes confrontation from relationship building. The update must identify which interests changed, how that altered a particular offer, and where his more ambitious cultural inferences fail.
2. **“China moved from relationships to institutions.”** Solomon already describes restored bureaucracies and technical issues; Freeman discusses professional specialization and learning. The actual question is who now prepares, authorizes and implements a choice, and what those changes enable or obstruct.
3. **“China learned to accept detailed commitments after accession.”** The existing 1991 border and 1999 accession records contradict an undifferentiated early-vagueness baseline. The later development to establish is the scale, routinization and deployment of expertise, not its first appearance.
4. **“Greater power produced one harder Xi-era style.”** The current cases differ simultaneously in issue, relationship and available instrument. They can refute a categorical claim, but cannot isolate the effect of relative power or personal leadership. The [security literature audit](../literature/readings/security-change.md) also warns against dating all capability expansion to Xi.

Calling an observed tactic non-unique is a useful comparative correction, but not a refutation of Solomon by itself: he distinguishes distinctive practices from unique ones. The stronger test concerns their prevalence, combination, conditions and effects. Equally, finding that agreements continue to be discussed does not validate every component of his implementation claim.

## Where the proposition test is uneven

The two [claim](../book/context-claims.json) [registers](../book/process-claims.json) are a sound baseline, but they are not yet a completed assessment. Repeated mentions in different syntheses are not additional tests.

**Most developed:** document specificity and principles (S-P05/S-C13); partial settlement (S-P12); prior-record disputes (S-P19); senior authority (S-P24/S-C09–12); implementation bargaining (S-P25); interests and relationship purpose (S-C01/S-C18). Even here several propositions are conjunctive. Partial settlement does not establish face-saving language; invocation does not establish favorable reinterpretation; continuing talks do not establish asymmetric adherence demands.

**Important but insufficiently developed:** deadlines and delay (S-P11/S-P23/S-C16); publicity and domestic audiences (S-P15/S-P17–18/S-C17); coercion (S-P13); learning and specialized practices (F07/F09/F13–14). Existing accounts often document activity or institutional assignment while leaving its effect unresolved. The book needs a small number of decisions with enough evidence to test the mechanism, not more general assertions that the mechanism is plausible.

**The most consequential substantive omission is “old friends.”** The later cases have not reconstructed the complete chain implied by S-C04/S-C06 and S-P02/S-P14: choosing a sympathetic foreign intermediary, making access or reputation conditional, the intermediary's subsequent behavior, and a changed government instruction or offer. Commercial lobbying in the agricultural files demonstrates access and advocacy, but not that China cultivated and pressured the advocate. Senior meetings and friendly language cannot fill this gap. At minimum one later US episode needs a serious test, including the possibility that the tactic failed or that the intermediary had little authority.

Other largely untested propositions concern hospitality and venue, speaking first, humor, domestic reporting, informal instructions, mentoring and knowledge of foreign politics. Do not turn each into a new chapter. Place all 59 propositions in a final appendix with a current judgment, strongest supporting and contrary evidence, and an explicit untested designation where warranted. The main text should explain why certain once-salient observations now receive less explanatory weight. Lack of a test is not proof of disappearance.

The introduction should open with a documented disagreement over what had actually been promised, then state the book's explanation and scope. The conclusion should answer what persists, what changed and where the tests remain inconclusive, with a bounded September 2026 endpoint. Later 2025–2026 trade developments belong in the implementation chapter and conclusion where their instrument stage can be stated accurately. A closing date does not require pretending that every negotiation in that year has been studied.

This design avoids adding complete chapters on every potential case. Hong Kong is a baseline challenge; WTO law and climate supply the middle-period bridge; one earlier and one recent US crisis are essential scope tests; India and Russia constrain generalization. Further cases earn inclusion only if they can overturn a current inference or supply an otherwise missing mechanism.

## Current wording and status issues to resolve in integration

These are not allegations of fabricated reading. Several are sensible historical notes that need an authoritative current counterpart.

- The 8 September trade table says S-P25 is retained strongly. The 10 September assessment correctly limits support to the continuing-arena component and leaves asymmetric adherence unresolved. The manuscript must use the later split judgment. Preserve the dated earlier memo as research history, with a clear route to the current judgment.
- Several tables call S-C18 both a retained explanation and an explanation still to test. “Interests matter” is compatible with almost every outcome. Retain it as Solomon's warranted scope restriction; a causal explanation in a chapter requires the specified cost, alternative and decision evidence.
- The [README](../README.md) calls other counterparts necessary controls. The actual comparisons are selected contrasts with multiple changing conditions. Use “comparisons” unless the specific variables held constant are stated; the EU accession comparison is closer but still has sequencing and institutional complications.
- The [bibliography](../literature/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) calls the implementation assessment the latest despite the Russian reassessment. Individual entries sometimes retain “public route identified” after a complete reading exists in the current ledger. These do not overturn the ledger's precise version claims, but manuscript notes and reader navigation need one current source of status.
- In the [implementation assessment](../cases/implementation-and-major-power-reassessment.md), the paragraph beginning “Its Annex 16” follows discussion of the 2017 plan and hearing. Name **Phase One's Annex 16** explicitly; the antecedent otherwise risks attaching a 2020 provision to the 2017 bargain.
- The [first literature assessment](../literature/first-full-text-synthesis.md) describes the then-bounded Chadha reading; the ledger correctly records later completion. That is a dated progression, not a contradiction to erase. Manuscript citations must use the scope and version actually supporting their present claim.
- The new manuscript needs a single current claim-assessment appendix. The baseline JSON should remain the record of what Solomon and Freeman said; it should not silently become the final verdict register. The protocol already calls for new propositions when needed, but the positive 2026 argument still needs separately stated propositions and disconfirmation conditions.

## Publication gates that follow from this particular argument

**Argument gate.** The introduction must state at least two empirically supported developments after the original period, distinguish them from corrections to the historical baseline, and specify where they alter bargaining. “More complexity” and “context matters” are insufficient. Each substantive chapter must answer a question that the preceding chapter left open and finish with a conclusion earned by its own evidence.

**Historical bridge gate.** The manuscript cannot claim to update negotiation with the United States through 2026 while omitting the accession-to-trade-war interval and acute US crises. It need not chronicle every administration, but it must contain the institutional and crisis comparisons specified above, or clearly change its title and claim to the narrower scope actually supported. Narrowing is not the present user objective.

**Access gate.** No chapter may dramatize an unavailable meeting, attribute a negotiator's unrecorded thoughts, or make a public white paper perform the work of a private instruction. Where access is asymmetric, narrate the disagreement between records and make the author's inference visible. Public records and selective retrospective collections can support a serious book; claiming Solomon-equivalent interviews or private access cannot.

**Causal gate.** For every claim that leadership, pressure, nationalism, friendship or learning caused a concession, identify the observed change in the offer, evidence that the proposed mechanism preceded and reached the decision, and the strongest live alternative. If this evidence fails, preserve the important descriptive finding and lower the causal claim. An entire book of lowered claims still needs a positive explanation supported by the best-documented episodes.

**Reader gate.** Current memos frequently lead with what cannot be inferred. A chapter should lead with what happened, what the actors disagreed about and why it matters; present the consequential uncertainty at that point, with detailed source criticism in notes. Do not transfer every source-count, hash, read-scope declaration and acquisition failure into the prose. Preserve them in the apparatus where they make the argument auditable.

**Integration gate.** Deliver a complete manuscript with stable chapter order, a single current proposition assessment, chronological and source consistency across chapters, complete endnotes and bibliography, and a conclusion that answers the question posed in the introduction. Independent reviews must test both scholarship and readability, including a reader who has not followed the research. Passing file or link checks only proves the apparatus works; it does not prove the book's explanation.

The next step is drafting and adversarial review while closing these finite gaps. More research earns its place by changing a chapter's answer. Another general synthesis, another total of sources read, or another unexecuted plan would leave the central work unfinished.

## Review addendum: manuscript and new cases, 15 September 2026

The [introduction](chapters/00-introduction.md) and [accession chapter](chapters/02-accession.md) now constitute substantive first manuscript drafts. The accession chapter makes an argument through dated papers and sector-specific bargaining rather than merely announcing further research. This changes the drafting status recorded earlier in this audit; it does not satisfy the whole-book publication gates.

The reviewed revisions distinguish fixed calendar deadlines from accession-relative periods, life-insurance ownership from telecommunications recollections, and auto-finance movement from its incompletely recovered authorization chain. The signed November 15 body establishes agreement and onward multilateral work without supplying its missing annexes. The introduction accurately bounds the 2014/2025 compliance-rule changes and the eight-month acceleration associated with Phase One. It no longer makes institutions or technical precision discoveries that Solomon and Freeman failed to recognize.

The European integration supplies an unsuccessful March round alongside May agreement. Independent checking of the Chinese history's printed pp. 383–385 and the WTO schedule supports an important distinction: Zhu's confirmation of a retained limit was something Lamy requested before acceptance; the precise timing and cause of renewed European authority remain unproved. The comparison preserves sequencing, different commercial priorities and member-state mandates. Three final corrections were checked in the revised manuscript: a scheduled opening is not evidence of actual implementation; a ban on foreign majority ownership is not necessarily Chinese managerial control; and the Commission's October account attributes to China a complaint about burdens beyond those normally required of an acceding country, not specifically a breach of its bilateral bargains. The [European case](../cases/eu-wto-2000-comparison.md) and its 23 move records were also reviewed; this is a check of the authored argument and its consequential supporting passages, not a claim to have independently read every listed source in full.

The [climate case](../cases/climate-2009-2015.md) now supplies a usable 2009–2015 bridge. Its revisions correctly identify increasing specification of procedures, distinguish Copenhagen's note-only disposition from partial political agreement, and separate Paris adoption, later consent, procedural obligations and national target achievement. Its source register distinguishes full documents from selected browser captures. Selected move-ledger entries were also checked. This is evidence of institutional development and changing agreement design, with learning and the causes of Chinese acceptance still to test; it is not a climate implementation history through 2026.

The present verdict is **ready for continued manuscript integration and targeted research, not publication**. The full US and EU annexes and internal instructions remain material gaps within the accession chapter, to be exposed in its claims rather than allowed to prevent all drafting. Whole-book requirements still include the US crisis comparison, a direct test of the old-friends mechanism, a decision-level test of acquired expertise, the later climate/implementation boundary, a current assessment of all 59 baseline propositions, and a complete comparative conclusion. Neither these first chapters nor the new case files establish a single causal trajectory from the normalization period to 2026. No new interviews or private negotiating archives were obtained.
