## Argument and scope

The organizing question is how political authority becomes a commitment that a counterpart can use. Relationships help establish purpose and access; specialized institutions define, authorize and implement particular terms; costs and alternatives limit the bargain. The manuscript must explain when that interaction changed an offer or its implementation. It must not turn a list of relevant factors into a claim that the causes of change have already been isolated.

There are two separate comparisons. The historical comparison asks how instruments and capabilities developed after the original normalization period. The counterpart comparison asks why behavior varies across relationships and issues. Greater Chinese power, different American demands, new regulatory tools, changed institutional knowledge and the preferences of particular leaders cannot be treated as interchangeable causes.

The intended scale is a seven-chapter analytical monograph with introduction and conclusion, provisionally around 55,000–70,000 words before notes. This is a planning range to contain sprawl, not a completion target. The user’s goal is a book-quality update; neither a source count nor reaching a word count establishes that result.

## The finite remaining research priorities

1. **US crisis bargaining:** obtain the actual 2001 messages and relevant recent demands/readouts before committing to a paired causal explanation. The original book’s confrontation caveat makes this essential.
2. **The old-friends mechanism after normalization:** reconstruct a favored intermediary, an inducement or threatened loss of access, the intermediary’s response and any resulting instruction. A finding that the channel had little influence is valuable; a list of meetings is insufficient.
3. **Expertise reaching a decision:** develop the completed WTO dispute-and-implementation sequence and climate comparison into the chapter’s comparative argument. Formal training or compliance rules establish institutions; they do not independently establish effect.
4. **Accession and implementation gaps:** pursue the identified annexes, authority records and denominators. If unavailable after a bounded search, retain a narrower finding inside the full-scope book, clearly distinguish the missing evidence, and continue. An inaccessible private file must not become an indefinite prerequisite for all writing.
5. **The present-day endpoint:** update each modern case’s event cutoff individually. A September access date does not mean its entire history is current to September. The final chapter must identify which 2026 announcements became operative instruments and which remain proposals or negotiations.

## Required apparatus and review

The baseline [59 Solomon/Freeman propositions](../book/context-claims.json) [and process propositions](../book/process-claims.json) stay unchanged as records of what the authors said. A separate current assessment appendix is still required: actual wording and scope, tested component, verdict, strongest evidence and contrary evidence, and the untested remainder. New propositions about the present must have their own evidentiary tests. Repetition across cases does not count as independent corroboration.

The finished manuscript also requires consistent endnotes, a consolidated bibliography, a chronology, clear source provenance, and a readable document export after the prose is stable. The full source texts stay outside Git. No new interviews, correspondence or private archive access have been conducted.

Before the goal can be marked complete, every chapter must exist and advance the argument; the missing historical bridge and US crisis comparison must be addressed; consequential causal claims must survive independent challenge; dates, figures and source chains must agree across chapters; and a reader unfamiliar with the dossier must be able to follow the book without consulting its working notes. File validation checks the apparatus, not the historical explanation.
