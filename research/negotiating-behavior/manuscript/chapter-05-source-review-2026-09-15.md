# Chapter 5 source review: tariff timing and selected bilingual clauses

Independent review, 15 September 2026. This memo checks the existing [agreement audit](../sources/phase-one-agreement-audit.md) against the published English and Chinese texts, and adds two preserved tariff notices. It does not review the chapter manuscript, reconstruct missing drafts, or determine performance.

## Tariff timing

The [supplementary register](../sources/phase-one-tariff-sequencing-register.json) records **POT-01**, 84 FR 69447, and **POT-02**, 85 FR 3741, with full target-notice readings, visual checks, exact dates and hashes. POT-01 was filed December 13 and published December 18, 2019; it suspended the planned December 15 duties from their scheduled start and expressly left a reduction of existing Annex A duties to a further notice. POT-02 was published January 22, 2020 and prescribed a reduction from 15 to 7.5 percent effective February 14. A December announcement, suspension of a planned increase, and a subsequent rate reduction are distinct events. The January notice supplies a prospective step within a January narrative, not evidence of February customs performance.

## Bilingual findings

The selected clauses support the existing audit's principal distinctions. No translation difference found here reverses the numerical obligations or converts the dispute procedure into independent adjudication. Three wording nuances and one omitted exit provision merit care.

| Provision and exact locators | Finding for drafting |
|---|---|
| **1.34–1.35:** POA-EN PDF 19, printed 1-18; POA-CN PDF 18–19, printed 1-17–1-18 | Both versions preserve choice of domestic implementation methods and require full implementation. Article 1.34 refers to the **Agreement**, despite its placement in the IP chapter; 1.35's action plan concerns the chapter. English 1.35 says “promulgate”; Chinese says **制定**, meaning formulate or draw up. Neither version makes the plan optional. Keep the common 30-working-day period after entry into force, measures and effective dates; do not silently present the verbs as identical wording for a public-posting deadline. The existing audit's neutral description of a required action plan is sound. |
| **6.2:** POA-EN PDF 53–54, printed 6-1–6-2; POA-CN PDF 74–76, printed 6-1–6-3 | Both make the scheduled figures increments above 2017 baselines, and both characterize 2022–2025 growth as a projection (**双方预测**). The Chinese annual amounts must be read with their preceding baseline phrase and the annex's explicit **增量** heading. Chinese 6.2.4 explicitly refers to sufficient US goods and services (**有足够的**); English refers to facilitating availability. Both require appropriate US steps, without specifying an independently quantified supply guarantee or automatic Chinese exemption. Market-based pricing, commercial considerations and purchase timing do not expressly cancel the minima. |
| **Annex 6.1:** POA-EN PDF 55, printed 6-3; POA-CN PDF 77–78, printed 6-4–6-5 | The agricultural increments agree: $12.5 billion and $19.5 billion. English note b's “will strive” and Chinese note 2's **将每年尽量增加** both distinguish the additional $5 billion annually from the scheduled minima. The services notes also agree on the inclusion of commercial-presence supply for financial, insurance and cloud services. This check covers the summary table and notes, not every detailed commodity entry. |
| **7.4:** POA-EN PDF 77–79, printed 7-2–7-4; POA-CN PDF 82–83, printed 7-3–7-4 | Both versions condition unilateral action on the specified process, preserve proportionality, and assign the good-/bad-faith assessment to the respondent. English “may not” and Chinese **不会** differ literally, but the latter does not establish a separate permission to counterrespond after accepting good faith. Both specify written withdrawal if the respondent considers the action bad faith. Neither supports an unconditional, uniquely Chinese waiver or automatic tariff restoration. The confidentiality and urgent-escalation provisions remain intact. |
| **8.3:** POA-EN PDF 82, printed 8-1; POA-CN PDF 87, printed 8-1 | Both separately permit either party to terminate by written notice, normally effective after 60 days or another agreed date. Add this to any account of the exit structure: bad faith is not the only available basis for leaving. Article 7.4 uses withdrawal/**退出** and supplies no separate effective-date formula; 8.3 uses termination/**终止**. State each provision rather than claiming the text expressly resolves every interaction between them. Entry into force is within 30 days of signature or the specified completed-procedure notifications, whichever is sooner, not unconditional effectiveness on signature. |

Article 8.6 makes both language versions equally authentic. The differences identified here are reasons to preserve the wording, not evidence of deliberately inconsistent bargains or permission to select whichever version favors one party. No new interpretive authority or subsequent party practice was examined to resolve these nuances.

The selected English and Chinese clauses and summary notes were read completely. Consequential English and Chinese table, remedy and IP wording, Chinese termination wording, and both tariff notices were visually inspected. The existing POA-EN/POA-CN register remains the source of agreement provenance; this review does not expand its full-work reading claim or revise shared files. The published English signature page contains blank signature spaces; the signing event has separate contemporaneous corroboration and should not be described as inspection of an executed original.
