# Ground systems: separating the platform, integrator and military appearance

Reviewed 15 September 2026. This sourcebook adds an attributable quadruped-platform claim to the earlier ground-robot cases. It does not identify every military quadruped, reconstruct weapon controls or estimate force-wide holdings.

## G01 — Five Eight Intelligent and the robot-wolf platform

**Finding:** 五八智能科技（杭州）有限公司, rendered here as Five Eight Intelligent Technology (Hangzhou), says its quadruped platform supported the robot-wolf system displayed in the 2025 parade. Its homepage separately identifies Q20A as the platform supporting the system at the 2024 Zhuhai airshow. This is direct company attribution, stronger than identifying a manufacturer from appearance. The precise contracting chain and 2025 configuration remain unverified.

**The sourced chain has two different levels of attribution:** official reporting establishes the military appearance; the company supplies the platform-attribution claim. The combination does not independently verify a purchase from Five Eight, establish that it supplied every subsystem, or identify all parade units as one commercial model. The 2024 institute name and later group attribution must be dated rather than treated as interchangeable corporate labels; see the [institutional map](institutions.md).

**Human role:** the 2024 signed article says complex activity still requires human operation and discusses greater autonomy prospectively. That is useful evidence about the author's assessment at that date. It does not establish the 2026 software release, tested intervention performance or the allocation of authority in a specific fielded configuration. No current operator manual or acceptance test was reviewed.

**Industrial significance:** this makes a defense-connected commercialization route visible alongside the familiar private robotics companies. It is a reason to investigate the division between the mobile platform, system integration and supporting institution. It is not evidence that Five Eight is the exclusive supplier, that private platforms have no military connection, or that every robot called a wolf shares this lineage. In particular, it does not resolve the separate [2024 Golden Dragon exercise OEM question](../industrial-base/relationship-method.md).

## G02 — Capital and production claims have different denominators

Five Eight's [28 October 2025 financing announcement](https://www.58znkj.com/new-detail/37) reports approximately **CNY500 million** in first-round strategic financing, jointly led by 国新基金 and 浙江产投. It identifies pilot-facility construction, technical development and broader applications as intended uses. The same release describes a partner-supported annual capacity arrangement of 20,000 units/sets. These are **company-reported financing and capacity statements**, not annual output, PLA procurement, installed military capacity or audited revenue. Locator: opening financing paragraphs and “央企优势” section. The page's date is explicit; the financing close is described as recent, without an exact closing date.

The company markets multiple public-safety and industrial applications. Those markets may support platform development and production learning, but a commercial application is not automatically military adoption. A useful next record would reconcile factory output, shipments, revenue and end-market mix for a stated period; an investment amount cannot substitute for any of them.
