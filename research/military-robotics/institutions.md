# China's military robotics/autonomy institutions: six dated records

Research cutoff: 2026-09-15. This is a bounded institutional evidence map, not an exhaustive organization chart. Primary institutional publications and issuer disclosures establish the records below. An exchange-hosted credit-rating report is separately labelled as the rating agency's original assessment; it is not treated as a corporate registry extract. Publication date, event date and observation period are kept separate.

The useful analyst distinction is between a mandate, participation, an operating collaboration, an announced commercial agreement, and a legally completed ownership change. These records do not establish a central military robotics procurer. A research institute, university, defense conglomerate or industry consortium cannot be labelled that procurer merely because it works on autonomy.

## 1. The 2017 reorganization separates AMS, NDU and NUDT

**Event:** 2017-07-19; source published 2017-07-20. **Authority/status:** official account of Xi Jinping's establishment address, Xinhua dispatch reproduced by Beijing Daily and the Beijing municipal government. Historical mandate, not a complete current organization chart.

**Actors and evidenced roles:** 军事科学院 (Academy of Military Sciences, AMS) combines military theory and military technology research; 国防大学 (National Defense University, NDU) trains joint-command and senior/midlevel leadership personnel; 国防科技大学 (National University of Defense Technology, NUDT) combines military education and defense technology innovation. These are three separate institutions. AMS must also remain distinct from 中国科学院, the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

**Locator/short quote:** paragraphs beginning “习近平强调，军事科学院…”, “国防大学…” and “国防科技大学…”. AMS instruction: “军事理论和军事科技紧密结合”. The URL's 2019 migration path is not the event date. [Official speech account](https://tamgw.beijing.gov.cn/xinwendongtai/dqdt/201912/t20191228_1536872.html).

**Unknown:** contemporary subordinate-unit chart, purchasing delegations and robotics budget allocation. **Analyst inference:** AMS is a research actor and NUDT a research/education actor; neither label establishes a monopoly over autonomy development or procurement.

## 2. AMS Defense Technology Innovation Institute has reported ties to NUDT and force users

**Observation:** reported 2025-11-24; individual events described only as recent/autumn. **Authority/status:** PLA Daily institutional reporting by 何焱 and 贾启龙; source-attributed research and exercise participation.

**Evidenced relationships:** 军事科学院国防科技创新研究院 led an unnamed system's development and its inclusion in an unnamed Army brigade exercise involving manned/unmanned collaboration. Separately, the article reports an institute-initiated joint research effort involving 国防科技大学 and military units, and describes recurring NUDT–force research exchanges. The article does not establish that these are the same project.

**Locator/short quote:** PLA Daily 2025-11-24, printed p.2, article “全军科研院所向战为战奋进攻关”; opening paragraph and paragraph beginning “各级科研院所探索推行…”. Short quote: “跨院所、跨军种联合科研攻关”. [Original newspaper page](https://rmt-static-publish.81.cn/file/20251124/20f3dcdecb9085a5327022c681d2b0e2.pdf); [original article URL](https://www.81.cn/yw_208727/16423460.html).

**Unknown:** project identifiers, participating legal units, vendors, contracts, autonomy software and measured performance. **Analyst inference:** there is a reported route from military research to force experimentation, but no named company or fielded robotic product can be attached from this report. Do not silently substitute AMS's separate 系统工程研究院 for 国防科技创新研究院.

## 3. NUDT's humanoid event documents private-sector contact, with a limited relationship stage

**Publication:** 2025-05-21; exact event day unspecified (“近日”). **Authority/status:** first-party NUDT event report; observed academic exchange/exhibition.

**Actors/relationship:** under the university research department's guidance, 国防科技大学智能科学学院 hosted a humanoid seminar and exhibition. Its “智行者” robot appeared alongside robots from 宇树科技, 乐聚机器人 and 逐际动力. College and company representatives gave presentations; the military robotics and industrial-development themes are explicit.

**Locator/short quote:** paragraphs 1–2 and “学术沙龙环节…”. Short quote: “开展学术交流及成果展示”. [NUDT event report](https://www.nudt.edu.cn/xwgg/hdss/d1d895112ef047a8834dcf487c5dddbd.htm).

**Unknown:** the source uses company/brand names rather than complete legal entities. It does not disclose a purchase, joint laboratory, software transfer, research contract, delivery, or deployment. **Analyst inference:** this is a real military–commercial contact point and a useful lead for subsequent disclosed agreements; coappearance is not a procurement edge. It does not establish that the civilian models' autonomous software was adopted by the PLA.

## 4. A joint unmanned-flight laboratory links a civilian university, a military university and an AVIC institute

**Event:** 2025-11-22, Xi'an; publication 2025-11-30. **Authority/status:** first-party 西北工业大学无人系统技术研究院 report of an operating laboratory's governance meeting.

**Actors/relationship:** 无人飞行器技术全国重点实验室 has three expressly identified co-building institutions: 西北工业大学, 空军工程大学 and 航空工业成都飞机设计研究所. Its academic committee's second plenary meeting heard the annual report and 2026 work plan and approved committee membership changes. This is stronger than a proposal to create a laboratory.

**Locator/short quote:** opening paragraph identifies “联合共建单位”; following paragraph refers to “三家共建单位”; final substantive paragraph records the annual report and committee decision. [Joint-laboratory meeting report](https://wurenxitong.nwpu.edu.cn/info/1107/11538.htm).

**Unknown:** founding date, legal incorporation, funding shares, project contracts, IP allocation and independently measured outputs. Other named attendees, including representatives of NUDT and other groups, are not automatically co-founders. 空军工程大学 is distinct from the separately listed 空军研究院 organizations. **Analyst inference:** this is a documented institutional bridge across academic, military and defense-industry research. It is not evidence that the lab purchases all unmanned aircraft or provides one common autonomy stack.

## 5. FiveEight: demonstrated industry affiliation and signed cooperation; exact legal transfer date remains unresolved

**Named actors:** 五八智能科技（杭州）有限公司; 中国兵器装备集团自动化研究所有限公司; 中国兵器装备集团有限公司 (CSGC); 中国兵器工业集团有限公司 (NORINCO group). The two group names are not synonyms. FiveEight is not identical to the automation institute merely because both appear in one institutional chain.

**2025-10-24 event:** FiveEight reports the opening of the Hangzhou humanoid pilot-testing base/application promotion center. It calls itself a NORINCO affiliate while identifying the automation institute chairman under the full CSGC-prefixed institute legal name. It also reports signed strategic cooperation with 襄阳长源东谷实业股份有限公司, 浙江海德曼智能装备股份有限公司 and 福立旺精密机电（中国）股份有限公司. **Stage:** opening and signatures reported; the component, manufacturing, supply-chain and standards cooperation described after signing is prospective. No completed joint product, component delivery or military acceptance is established. **Authority:** participant's first-party account. **Locator:** “70余名重要嘉宾共同见证” and “战略合作签约仪式”; short quote: “完成现场签约”. [FiveEight event report](https://58znkj.com/new-detail/36).

**2026-08-19–23 event / 2026-08-29 publication:** SASTIND places 中兵智能创新研究院, 杭州智元研究院 and “自动化所五八智能” among NORINCO's exhibiting organizations. It also reports the announced establishment of a NORINCO-led 中央企业机器人创新联合体. **Authority/status:** defense-industry regulator's first-party industry report; contemporary affiliation and consortium announcement, not a registry ownership certificate or PLA procurement mandate. **Locator:** paragraph beginning “兵器工业集团牵头布展央企展区…”. [SASTIND report](https://www.sastind.gov.cn/n10086200/n10086331/c10771822/content.html).

**Separate restructuring evidence:** 建设工业集团（云南）股份有限公司's H1 2026 report, published 2026-08-27, says it received notice on 2025-06-04 that the State Council approved splitting CSGC's automotive business into an independent central enterprise and injecting the remaining CSGC equity into NORINCO through the prescribed process. The issuer marks its previously disclosed planned control change as unfinished and reports no controller change in H1 2026. **Locator:** printed pp.28–29, 第六节/五 “控股股东或实际控制人变更情况”; short quote: “尚未完成”. This establishes that issuer's disclosure, not the transaction status of FiveEight or the automation institute. [Issuer report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-27/1225509473.PDF).

**Corroborative status caveat, not a registry source:** 中诚信国际信用评级有限责任公司's 2026-06-26 NORINCO follow-up rating says the CSGC injection remained incomplete, while the automotive split's registration had been completed by August 2025. **Locator:** printed p.6, “运营实力”, final three paragraphs. This is the rating agency's original contemporary assessment, not an issuer-authored legal opinion. [Exchange-hosted rating report](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/bond/announcement/company/c/new/2026-06-26/143112_20260626_TSIX.pdf).

**Unknown:** FiveEight's exact direct and indirect ownership percentages and transfer dates as of cutoff; whether group affiliation reflects administrative, business or completed equity integration at each date; partner deliverables and adoption. No verified primary ownership record in this bounded review establishes the widely repeated 60/40 joint-venture split, so it should not be imported. **Analyst inference:** report contemporary affiliation as company/regulator-attributed, preserve historical legal names, and track legal control separately. The sources do not require a forced reconciliation into one precise transfer date. NORINCO's industry consortium leadership does not establish central military buying authority.

## 6. HIT–Leju: a civilian university–company laboratory with a signed agreement and first meeting

**Event:** 2026-05-11; publication 2026-05-12. **Authority/status:** first-party Harbin Institute of Technology report; signed joint laboratory, unveiling and initial work meeting.

**Actors/relationship:** 哈尔滨工业大学计算学部 and 乐聚智能（深圳）股份有限公司 established a joint laboratory. The report identifies the company chairman/CTO as also a computing-division researcher, a specifically reported institutional affiliation rather than proof of a full-time employment contract. The agreement describes future collaboration in embodied manipulation, emotional interaction, care/companionship, commercialization and joint training. The launch also presented university-center outputs; these must not be relabelled as achievements of the newly created laboratory.

**Locator/short quote:** opening dated paragraph; paragraph beginning “计算学部人工智能学院执行院长…”; penultimate substantive paragraph. Short quote: “联合实验室举行首次工作会议”. [HIT report](https://computing.hit.edu.cn/2026/0512/c11270a391715/page.htm).

**Unknown:** funding, IP, deliverables, performance, military funding or a PLA customer. “乐聚机器人” at the NUDT event is a less precise brand-level identification than this legal name. **Analyst inference:** civilian research and commercial organizations can have documented, concrete relationships without the record establishing military transfer. Even after entity resolution, a firm appearing at both HIT and NUDT does not prove the HIT laboratory's software, models or data crossed into the military.

## Suggested graph treatment and analyst questions

The [artifact-lineage extension](lineage.md) provides research uses of named civilian software and a commercial component involving NUDT. These are stronger implementation links than attendance at the humanoid event, while still distinct from institution-wide adoption or accepted military equipment. Keep these research relationships separate from the six institutional records above.

Use dated relationship types: `historical_mandate`, `reported_joint_research`, `academic_exchange`, `joint_lab_governance`, `affiliation_reported`, `strategic_agreement_signed`, and `control_change_reported_pending`. Do not collapse these into one “military link” score. Keep the underlying Chinese institution name and source wording alongside an English display name.

These six records support a distributed research and commercialization network. They do not identify one master robotics organization, one shared autonomous software stack, one military customer, or automatic movement from a civilian laboratory to operational use. The next useful evidence would be a named contracting entity and award, a joint-lab project/deliverable, a published software or data licensing arrangement, or a corporate registry/issuer transaction-completion disclosure. Any individual university procurement notice would establish that buyer and transaction only.
