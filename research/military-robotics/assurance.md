# Qualification is several different decisions

Reviewed 15 September 2026. Public records make an export milestone and a civilian model's human-control requirements visible. They answer different questions from the [declared military human-control policy](../autonomy/doctrine.md).

**Ask what was qualified, by whom, for which use, and with what evidence.** Military-export approval, civil type certification, customer acceptance and a tested autonomous function should have separate records. These cases do not establish a public PLA autonomy acceptance protocol.

## QA01 — Wing Loong-X: reported export approval before sales

**中航（成都）无人机系统股份有限公司 / AVIC (Chengdu) UAS Co., Ltd.** reported in May 2025 that **Wing Loong-X / 翼龙-X** had first flown in 2024 and completed military-export qualification and obtained export approval in 2025. It was then seeking customers. [Issuer investor-relations record, Q1, PDF p.2](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H22_AN202506061685876649_1.pdf). The document specifies May meetings without a day; the disclosure is indexed on 6 June.

The [November 2025 record, no. 2025-04, Q5–7, PDF p.2](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H22_AN202512051794473733_1.pdf) repeats the approval, describes the product as saleable and forecasts sales in 2026. This demonstrates the distinction in the issuer's own reporting: approval was achieved while sales were still prospective. The forecast is not a verified subsequent result.

No original approval instrument, named approving agency, evaluator, certificate number or test criteria was obtained. The strongest claim is **issuer-reported military-export qualification and approval**. It does not establish a PLA customer or the authority assigned to an autonomous function. Two management records are repeated corporate evidence, not independent certification evidence.

## QA02 — Wing Loong-2 meteorological model: concrete civil human-control conditions

CAAC published **SC-92-001** for the **翼龙-2气象型无人驾驶航空器系统** on **10 February 2025**, effective that day and listed as valid. The applicant is the same issuer; the civil reviewer is the Southwest Regional Administration. The application dates to 1 March 2023, accepted the following day under NATC0158A. [Official listing](https://www.caac.gov.cn/XXGK/XXGK/BZGF/ZYTJHHM/202502/t20250210_226667.html); [final conditions, preliminary pp.I–III](https://www.caac.gov.cn/XXGK/XXGK/BZGF/ZYTJHHM/202502/P020250210615770996508.pdf).

The final text supplies requirements with concrete human roles:

| Provision | What the condition requires | Printed / physical PDF page |
|---|---|---|
| WL.1907 | Determine minimum operators from workload and access to controls. | 114 / 128 |
| WL.1967 | If automatic takeoff/landing is installed, allow operators to interrupt it readily and quickly. | 121 / 135 |
| WL.2025–2029 | Identify the controlling ground station/workstation, preserve control through handover, and address staffing and unambiguous controls for multi-aircraft operation. | 127–128 / 141–142 |

These are model-specific **civil certification requirements**, not observed test results or a published operator-to-aircraft ratio. The official final instrument is distinct from the earlier consultation draft.

In its [19 August 2026 H1 action-plan assessment, PDF p.2](https://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESH_STOCK/2026/2026-8/2026-08-19/12501660.PDF), the issuer says the meteorological model completed all conformity-verification work and was ready to enter the final certification stage. It does not announce an issued type certificate. This review obtained neither that regulator decision nor individual test reports. The statement describes company-reported progress through the first half of 2026.

**The comparison sharpens the doctrine question.** Public civil documents can specify staffing and interruption at the level of an integrated aircraft, ground station and link. Establishing analogous implementation in a military system requires its own evidence. A shared manufacturer or platform family cannot transfer the scope of this civil instrument to a military variant.

## What an analyst should collect next

| Existing evidence | The next record that would change the assessment |
|---|---|
| A policy requiring human control | A system-specific requirement assigning authorization, interruption and review responsibilities. |
| A model-specific requirement | A dated test/conformity record identifying the evaluated configuration and result. |
| An issuer's qualification claim | The authority's decision and its exact scope. |
| Customer acceptance | Continuing-use evidence, including updates, exceptions and support work. |

These are different evidence categories; they need not occur in a single uniform sequence. The [revenue study](scale.md) also shows why accounting acceptance and shipment should not be treated as tests of autonomous performance.

## Search and access limits

The September search combined named-system queries with military testing, qualification, finalization and acceptance terms across military reporting, procurement, issuer disclosures, CAAC, SSE and SASTIND. Generic test-service offers, proposed evaluation architectures and standards titles were excluded as proof of a passed test. No matched public record was obtained identifying a PLA system, its autonomous function, accepting body and observed acceptance result. This does not establish that such testing is absent.

The final CAAC PDF was downloaded from its official host and relevant provisions read; its cover and printed pp.114/121 were visually inspected. The issuer's two investor-relations PDFs and August assessment were downloaded from financial-disclosure mirrors; original issuer/SSE copies were not obtained. The November approval paragraph was visually inspected. Dates and locators refer to those reviewed versions. Full copyrighted documents are not redistributed.
