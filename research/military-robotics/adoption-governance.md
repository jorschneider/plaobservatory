# How equipment development becomes an accepted and supported purchase

Reviewed 15 September 2026. This institutional map extends the [procurement records](procurement.md), [qualification comparison](assurance.md) and [institutional map](institutions.md).

**The public framework assigns different work to research management, ordering, contract supervision, quality assurance and equipment support.** A promising robot, a selected supplier and an accepted configuration therefore answer different institutional questions. The strongest new evidence is an accessible quality regulation that connects contracts, software evaluation, contributors' responsibilities and acceptance. Newer announcements establish additional frameworks without exposing their full operating provisions.

## AG01 — Research approval and ordering are separate records

The revised **《军队装备科研条例》**, announced by Xinhua on **5 February 2025** and effective **1 March 2025**, covers planning, project approval, management differentiated by project category and level, quality/cost controls and research acceptance assessment. This is the existing research-governance anchor in [procurement.md](procurement.md#a-regulation-announcement-is-an-institutional-anchor-not-the-operating-rulebook). The announcement supplies neither the full regulation nor a robotics-specific assessment. [S1]

The **《军队装备订购规定》**, announced and effective **1 November 2021**, separately identifies a management sequence: **requirements generation → planning → project establishment → contracting → fulfillment supervision**. Its announcement locates the framework within the CMC's overall direction, theater commands' operational focus and services' force-building responsibilities. It does not identify the approving office or buyer for a particular project. [S2]

These are announcements of promulgated military regulations, not commentary. The underlying clauses were not obtained. They support distinct research and acquisition records, without proving that every robotics purchase follows an identical research-to-order sequence.

## AG02 — A contract is followed by supervision and acceptance

The **《军队装备采购合同监督管理暂行规定》**, announced **19 March 2022**, effective **20 March**, establishes a separate contract-supervision framework. The announcement describes supervision tasks, agreements and plans, combining prevention, management during performance and subsequent evaluation. Its stated delivery objective is qualified equipment; it does not report a delivered robot or reveal project-level sign-off powers. [S3]

The enacted **《武器装备质量管理条例》**, promulgated **30 September 2010**, effective **1 November**, is available in full: [S4]

| Institutional distinction | Public provision |
|---|---|
| Contract versus test result | Article 17 requires contractual quality responsibilities, standards and acceptance criteria/methods. |
| Integrator versus component supplier | Articles 18/33 assign quality coordination to the overall-task/prime contractor and purchased/outsourced-product responsibility to the developer/producer. |
| Software development versus approved change | Article 22 requires independent evaluation of performance/safety-relevant software; article 23 requires approval and verification for specified configuration changes. |
| Delivery versus supported use | Article 39 assigns military representatives inspection/acceptance against contract and technical requirements, plus supervision of user/maintainer training. |

The historical **General Armaments Department / 总装备部** name is retained. These provisions establish requirements, not a present organizational assignment or project compliance.

## AG03 — A 2026 draft explicitly addresses software iteration

SASTIND's **13 July 2026** notice says it and the CMC Equipment Development Department jointly prepared a revision of the 2010 quality regulation, inviting comments through **13 August**. The retrieved attachment remains a **consultation draft**; article 65 contains a placeholder effective date. No later promulgation was located in this bounded review. [S5]

The draft proposes software classification and corresponding quality requirements in article 8 and digital engineering data and product-model controls in article 33. Its attached explanation, III(3), expressly connects the proposals to agile software iteration. Article 19 retains independent software evaluation; that principle already appears in the 2010 text. The product-model provision does not specify an autonomy-model evaluation protocol. These are proposals, not implemented requirements. [S5]

## AG04 — Acceptance is followed by equipment support

The **《军队装备保障条例》**, announced **9 November 2024**, effective **1 December**, addresses equipment support. Its announcement describes functional boundaries among support departments and processes for **supply, management and repair**. The full 92-article regulation was not obtained. The announcement establishes a support framework, without identifying a named system's maintainer, spare-parts contract, software-update authority or achieved availability. [S6]

This adds an institutional reason to follow a system beyond its first contract. The relevant analytical outcome is sustained useful service, which requires separate operating and support evidence.

## Preserve the purchasing route

A separate CMC Logistics Support Department instrument, **《军队物资工程服务采购合同管理暂行规定》**, took effect **3 January 2022**. The **13 January** MOD publication of the PLA Daily announcement assigns purchasing units responsibility for supervising fulfillment and describes delivery acceptance, payment/settlement and performance evaluation. It covers goods, works and services; the full instrument was not obtained. [S7]

The applicable route is a fact to establish for each notice. A military buyer purchasing a commercial robot or operated flight service does not, by itself, identify the governing equipment instrument or a military representative as accepting authority. Civil certification and military-export approval remain separate decisions, as the [qualification study](assurance.md) shows.

## What changes the analyst's work

**Analytical inference:** commercial reuse can avoid originating every component while leaving integration, evaluation and support work with the downstream organization. The [lineage cases](lineage.md) identify reusable artifacts; this dossier identifies the institutional questions that follow. Neither set measures the cost or duration of that transition. Configuration governance could affect iteration, but its practical effect requires a matched project history.

For a named project, collect the research sponsor and approval; ordering authority and contract; overall-task/prime integrator; assessed configuration and accepting signatory; operator and support organization. Keep missing roles unknown. A matched chain would explain how an experiment became an accepted, maintained system; a list of promulgated regulations cannot substitute for it.

The unresolved questions are concrete: which framework applies to each robotics purchase, who holds the relevant delegated authority, what criteria cover the automated function, what changes trigger reassessment, and what continuing support was delivered. These sources provide no national conversion rate from demonstrations to adoption, no uniform procurement duration, and no evidence that human-authority policies have been implemented in a particular robot.
