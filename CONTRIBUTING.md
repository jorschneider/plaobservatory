# Contributing

Corrections, stronger primary sources, identity resolutions, and disconfirming evidence are welcome. The project values claim precision over biography length.

## A useful correction includes

- Stable officer ID, position ID, claim ID, adverse ID, or research-gap ID when one exists.
- Exact field or sentence that should change.
- Original source URL and publisher.
- Publication date and event/observation date if different.
- Exact Chinese name and title as printed.
- A short explanation of what the source supports.
- A short explanation of what the source does not support.
- Archived copy or content hash when a mutable page can be preserved lawfully.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer formal Party/state decisions and official Chinese military or institutional sources.
- Do not treat a search-result snippet as evidence.
- Do not infer current incumbency from an undated roster or old biography.
- Do not infer patronage from shared geography, school, or unit service without a dated working relationship.
- Do not infer operational effectiveness from presence at an exercise or from unit performance without attribution.
- Flag conflicting sources instead of silently choosing the preferred one.
- State uncertainty directly; unknown values do not count as favorable evidence.
- An acting label needs literal acting language in the source (代, 代理, 主持工作, 主持日常工作, 履行职责). Without it, an officer seen running things is a handler.

## Data changes

The files under `research/` are the inputs. `app/data/observatory.json` and everything under `public/data` are generated by `node scripts/build-v8-data.mjs`. Do not edit the generated files by hand; a pull request that changes them without a matching change under `research/` will be rejected by the canonical-build test.

Where each kind of change goes:

- A new position, or a new holder, handler or adverse link on an existing seat: `research/positions.json`. An officer who fits no seat goes in `unmappedOfficers` with a reason from `unmappedReasons` and a note. The build fails on an unknown officer or adverse ID, and on an active officer who is neither mapped nor listed with a reason.
- A ledger date (last public appearance, first concrete signal, formal action, or an intermediate action): `research/adverse-timeline.json`. Every date needs a URL, a `kind` from `signalKinds`, a source class, and the exact Chinese title in the note. Leave a date null rather than guess it; the build computes the day counts and the collection state.
- A tracker row: the relevant file under `research/trackers/`. Names are matched to the dataset by exact Chinese name.
- A removal found by a tracker that is not in the ledger: `research/adverse-additions.json`, with the official notice as its `formalAction` and a `discoveredBy` label. The build rejects an addition whose ID or Chinese name matches an active officer.
- A judgment, premise or research question: `app/data/assessment.ts`. Every headline record needs a `plain` sentence a non-specialist can follow and, where a claim is made, an `example` with a date and a URL. A judgment may only use numbers that the interface fills from `metadata`, and may not depend on a retired premise.
- A review entry: `research/review-log.json`. docs/REVIEW_LOG.md is the readable copy and should be updated to match.

Before submitting a pull request, run:

```bash
npm ci
node scripts/build-v8-data.mjs
npm test
npm run lint
```

The tests enforce canonical/public data equality under one build ID, adverse-ledger separation, stable population accounting, position mapping for every active officer, clock ordering and day arithmetic, plain-language and premise-register rules, and known factual corrections.

## Security and privacy

Use only lawfully available public information. Do not submit personal contact information, private credentials, hacked material, or non-public personal data. The project concerns public professional roles and institutional analysis.
